# Mira Genesis — commercial licensing and acquisition enquiries

Mira Genesis contains a public research history and may also develop future proprietary or
restricted-use components.

## Historical public software

Software already published in this repository under `AGPL-3.0-only` remains available under that
licence. The AGPL permits commercial activity subject to its terms. **No separate commercial licence
is required merely to exercise rights that an existing AGPL-covered copy already grants.**

This document does not revoke, narrow or replace those existing rights.

## Future and separately controlled material

For material that is not already covered by an irrevocably granted public licence and for which the
project controls the rights needed to do so, separate commercial arrangements may be considered,
including:

- alternative commercial licensing terms;
- proprietary product components;
- hosted or managed services;
- integration, support and validation services;
- access to unpublished R&D or confidential know-how under contract;
- trademark licences;
- assignment or acquisition of controlled project assets.

Availability and scope depend on the rights actually controlled for the relevant material. Nothing
in this document promises that every historical repository component can be relicensed exclusively.

## Project acquisition

Enquiries about acquiring or licensing controlled Mira Genesis assets may include, subject to due
diligence and contract:

- copyright and relicensing rights actually held by the project owner;
- private source repositories and unpublished R&D;
- confidential technical know-how;
- product implementations and operational assets;
- trademark rights and registrations, if any at the time of the transaction;
- patent applications or patents, if any at the time of the transaction;
- documentation, provenance and reproducibility infrastructure.

Any transaction must identify the historical public assets and licences that continue to exist
independently of the transaction.

## Contact

Use the official GitHub repository owner / project-maintainer contact channel for commercial,
licensing or acquisition enquiries.

Do not send trade secrets, confidential task banks, credentials or other sensitive material through
a public issue or discussion.

## No implied rights

No patent, trademark, confidential-information or proprietary licence is granted by this document.
`TRADEMARKS.md`, `LICENSE_POLICY.md`, the applicable source licences and any signed agreement govern
the relevant rights.
