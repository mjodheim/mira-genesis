# Mira Genesis — commercial licensing and acquisition enquiries

Mira Genesis is intended to remain a public, auditable research project while preserving a separate
commercial-licensing path for proprietary use.

## Public licence

Software published in this repository under `LICENSE_POLICY.md` is available under
`AGPL-3.0-only`. The AGPL permits commercial activity subject to its terms.

**No separate commercial licence is required merely because an AGPL-compliant use makes money.**
This document does not revoke, narrow or replace rights already granted by the AGPL.

## Alternative commercial licence

For software for which **Anthony Mets controls the rights necessary to do so**, an alternative
commercial licence may be available under a separate written agreement.

A commercial agreement is intended for organisations that need rights beyond the public AGPL grant,
which may include, depending on the signed terms:

- proprietary or closed-source integration;
- keeping customer modifications or surrounding proprietary source confidential;
- proprietary redistribution, OEM or embedded use;
- commercially supported releases;
- integration and validation services;
- support, service levels, warranties or negotiated liability terms;
- access to separately controlled product components or unpublished material where offered.

The exact scope, fees, term, territory, warranties, liability, support and termination rules exist
only in the signed agreement. Mention of commercial licensing in this repository is an invitation to
negotiate, not an automatic grant of proprietary rights.

Third-party components remain governed by their own terms. A Mira Genesis commercial agreement can
only grant rights the project actually controls.

See [`docs/DUAL_LICENSING_STRATEGY.md`](docs/DUAL_LICENSING_STRATEGY.md) for the operating model.

## Public research and future milestones

Starting with M086, public research remains the normal publication path after a short IP/provenance
review. New research is not made private solely to preserve commercial licensing: public AGPL and
separate commercial licensing are compatible.

Temporary confidentiality may still be used when a specific reason has been recorded before
publication, for example patent-first review, intentional trade-secret treatment, confidential
third-party material, contractual embargo, security-sensitive material or a deliberately proprietary
product implementation.

## Project acquisition

Enquiries about acquiring or licensing controlled Mira Genesis assets may include, subject to due
diligence and contract:

- copyright and alternative-relicensing rights actually held by the project owner;
- the ability to continue offering commercial licences/exceptions for controlled material;
- private source repositories and unpublished R&D, if any;
- confidential technical know-how;
- product implementations and operational assets;
- trademark rights and registrations, if any at the time of the transaction;
- patent applications or patents, if any at the time of the transaction;
- commercial contracts and customer relationships, if any;
- documentation, provenance and reproducibility infrastructure.

Any transaction must identify historical/public AGPL and CC assets that remain independently
available under their existing licences after the transaction.

## Contact

Use the official GitHub repository owner / project-maintainer contact channel for commercial,
licensing or acquisition enquiries.

Do not send trade secrets, confidential task banks, credentials or other sensitive material through
a public issue or discussion.

## Before the first paid licence

The first binding customer agreement should be reviewed by a qualified Belgian/European IP lawyer.
This repository deliberately records the commercial model without improvising a universal customer
contract.

## No implied rights

No patent, trademark, confidential-information or proprietary commercial licence is granted by this
document. `TRADEMARKS.md`, `LICENSE_POLICY.md`, the applicable source licences and any signed
agreement govern the relevant rights.
