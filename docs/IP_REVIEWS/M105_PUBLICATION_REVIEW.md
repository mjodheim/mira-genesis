# M105 pre-publication review

**Decision date:** 24 August 2026  
**Project owner / research director:** Anthony Mets  
**Disposition:** `PUBLIC_AGPL_COMMERCIAL_OPTION`

This review is recorded before any enabling M105 runtime, executable constructor-feature language,
development fixture, qualification population, checker or result is created. The owner explicitly
authorised M105 and the continuing milestone sequence in the Codex task after M104 merged.

## Asset under review

M105 attacks the strongest measured ceiling left by positive M104: its constructor can acquire only
subsets of seven authored high-level feature identities, and the host interpreter assigns every
identity its semantics. M105 will test a narrower first removal of that ceiling. The exact positive
M104 V3 bytes will be wrapped unchanged in a successor state whose initially empty constructor-
feature registry can hold content-addressed executable classifier programs assembled from a fixed
lower Boolean expression substrate.

The DEVELOPMENT-only acquisition receives behavioral observations, not the accepted program body,
identifier or digest. A registered program must survive producer death and make later generalized
consumer definitions constructible beyond M104's complete finite exact-context dispatch image. A
fresh lineage with the same M104 predecessor, runtime, lower substrate, later observations and
complete semantic search, but no acquired feature bytes or DEVELOPMENT material, must remain unable
to validate the same later acquisition.

The materially new public mechanism is the executable constructor-feature representation, bounded
complete semantic enumerator, state-owned feature registry, generalized dependency-bearing consumer
definition and its causal controls. The lower Boolean operators, interpreter, two-signal context
contract, carrier adapters, tasks, evaluator and bounds remain authored and disclosed.

## Provenance and dependencies

Anthony Mets remains the sole human research director. OpenAI Codex supplies substantial research-
design, implementation, audit and documentation assistance as tooling, not human authorship. The
planned implementation uses the Python standard library, the repository's existing development
tooling and the already public M104/M103 runtime. SQLite may be exercised through Python's standard-
library binding as an independently maintained execution interface, not as independent task
authorship.

No external model, package, downloaded dataset, benchmark payload, confidential task bank,
credential, contractual embargo, network service, remote execution or new system authority is
introduced. If that changes, this review must be amended before the new material enters the public
apparatus.

## Rights and publication decision

No patent-first, intentional trade-secret, confidential-third-party, contractual, commercial-
private or security-sensitive reason for temporary private treatment is recorded. Project-
controlled software remains AGPL-3.0-only and research material remains under the repository's
documentation-licence policy. Alternative commercial permissions remain possible only for rights
controlled by the owner.

## Scientific boundary

M105 may support only bounded state-owned constructor-vocabulary extension if every decisive
condition qualifies. It cannot establish lower-interpreter ownership, self-hosting, recursive or
open-ended self-improvement, independent task authorship, a closed G1-G10 gate, general-agent
evidence or AGI. A negative attempt remains negative and must determine the next successor.

## Decision

Public pre-registration, implementation, final freeze, one canonical attempt, one frozen checker/
replay, result preservation, PR and merge are authorised under
`PUBLIC_AGPL_COMMERCIAL_OPTION`. This standing workflow authorisation does not permit changing a
frozen protocol after result materialisation or rerunning a consumed attempt.
