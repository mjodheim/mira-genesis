## Asset

M120/H65 is a prospective corrective successor to the closed M119/H64 record. M119 spent one
qualifying scientific generation, sealed and revealed once, and closed `instrument_aborted` with
H64 **untested**: zero carriers cleared the frozen qualification clauses, zero paired demands were
posed, and no arm ran.

M120 does not repair, rerun, reinterpret or relabel that observation. It inherits M119's scientific
proposition unchanged and inherits its arms, endpoint, exact test, alpha, effect floor, guards,
verdict states, decomposition, comparator seed, observation budget and admissibility minimums **by
import rather than by restatement**, with a mechanical check that refuses to derive an analysis
plan if those modules' bytes have moved.

What M120 discloses is instrument work:

- a carrier candidate contract whose schema states no relation between two fields, and a total,
  content-independent decoder that carries every schema-valid candidate into a carrier the frozen
  host accepts;
- a narrowing of the carrier family, chosen after reading M119's closed public bank and disclosed
  as such;
- a pre-seal scientific-adequacy gate with an enforced counts-only output allowlist;
- a re-measured DEVELOPMENT route-readiness gate for the new candidate schema, against a
  non-carrier stress schema that dominates its keyword census;
- a scoring entry point with no caller-selectable evidence path, which re-derives the analysis plan
  from code and reproduces the measurement from the committed carrier bank;
- a DEVELOPMENT end-to-end rehearsal in a disposable checkout, with nine adversarial substitutions.

## Scientific boundary

M120 is a new milestone and must receive a fresh H65 record.

- DEVELOPMENT probes are instrument tests, not scientific observations. They may use synthetic
  non-qualifying prompts and schemas; they **may not** send the H65 qualifying input, inspect a
  future H65 bank, or influence downstream scoring. The readiness gate's stress schema is
  deliberately a non-carrier schema for exactly this reason, and its result record carries no
  qualification statistic and no carrier count.
- The carrier family narrowing is an instrument-design dependency on M119's closed public bank and
  is disclosed in the preregistration, the analysis plan's limitations and the derivation report.
  It is not a selection over H65 outputs: the contract is fixed before generation and applies to
  every machine identically.
- A verdict here speaks about a **narrower carrier family** than M119 would have spoken about.
- M115 through M119 remain closed and immutable. The two M119 checker defects are requirements for
  this milestone, not permission to edit that one.
- No generality gate is advanced by anything in this milestone, and no AGI, recursive
  self-improvement or open-ended-intelligence claim is made or implied.
