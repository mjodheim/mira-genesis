## Status

This document is a **sanitized publication mirror**, not the original preregistration. The canonical v4 control plane was frozen on the private host before the v4 seed. This public copy is written after attempts 001 and 002 and does not retroactively alter their rules or evidence.

## Why v4 exists

The preceding free-mutation campaign produced several plausible, non-regressing descendants that remained on the same aggregate score plateau. Review identified three methodological limitations:

1. positive fitness was too tightly coupled to a fixed internal routing/profile ontology;
2. the proposer received too little empirical memory about what prior mutations had tried;
3. strict immediate hill climbing discarded neutral innovations that could still be useful empirical evidence.

v4 changes the **selection surface**, not the scientific authority boundary.

## External proposer

A fresh external proposer receives a bounded bundle containing:

- the retained parent organism;
- a content-addressed proposal context;
- integrity/orchestration metadata.

The proposer does not receive the private control repository, hidden evaluator cases, authority tests, evaluator logs or case-level expected/observed labels.

The proposer returns exactly one inert patch and one transcript. The model process does not run on the experiment host during evaluation.

External model competence is therefore a source of **variation**, not by itself evidence that the Genesis lineage owns that competence.

## Aggregate capability fitness

v4 evaluates a frozen capability surface as an aggregate vector with seven categories:

- `conversation`
- `personal_read`
- `code_delegation`
- `system_admin`
- `self_modify`
- `personal_write`
- `memory_media`

The evaluator measures required and forbidden capability exposure. Internal profile names are not themselves fitness, allowing descendants to change their internal organization without being rewarded or punished merely for renaming/restructuring profiles.

Only aggregate category scores are exposed to later proposers.

## Retention rule

A changed descendant can become the retained parent only if:

1. it passes the frozen hard external boundary;
2. it introduces no protected aggregate capability regression;
3. it produces at least one positive aggregate fitness signal — a category score improvement and/or an allowed warning reduction under the frozen policy.

Selection is Pareto-style: an improvement on one protected axis cannot compensate for a regression on another protected axis.

## Neutral archive

A descendant that:

- changes the organism;
- passes the hard boundary;
- produces no protected regression;
- produces no positive retention signal;

is **not inherited**, but may be preserved in a bounded content-addressed neutral archive.

The archive stores sanitized empirical memory such as its high-level hypothesis and aggregate outcome. A neutral candidate does not automatically become an ancestor and does not change the retained generation.

This creates an explicit distinction between:

- **retained evolutionary change**;
- **neutral empirical memory**;
- **rejected/regressing change**.

## Empirical memory

Later proposal contexts may include bounded aggregate history:

- attempt number;
- aggregate category scores and deltas;
- verdict;
- redacted proposer-authored intent/rationale/effects/uncertainties;
- content identities.

They do not expose hidden case details or raw evaluator outputs.

## Timing

Cross-runner raw wall-clock time is observable but **not a positive retention signal**. It is not used to prefer one descendant over another.

## Authority boundary

The candidate cannot rewrite the external evaluator, fitness policy, authority tests, campaign budget or canonical retained state. Evaluation occurs outside the mutable organism.

The laboratory controls:

- proposer input construction;
- proposal import;
- external evaluation;
- selection/adoption;
- lineage persistence;
- neutral archive persistence;
- construction of the next proposer context.

## What a retained generation means

A retained descendant demonstrates a bounded measured improvement on this frozen real-host capability surface while preserving protected dimensions.

A sequence of retained generations would additionally demonstrate **heredity**: later proposals are evaluated from an organism that already contains earlier retained changes.

One retained generation is not sufficient to establish open-ended evolution. Repeated retained generations with causal dependence on inherited changes would be a substantially stronger observation.

## Claim boundary

Free Metamorphosis v4 does not establish AGI, consciousness, autonomous production deployment, arbitrary self-programming, unrestricted repository authority, cross-project generality or open-ended recursive self-improvement.

The strongest claim available from this campaign remains bounded to the frozen host, capability surface, proposer interface, budgets and external authority boundary.
