# Genesis v2 — Open Metamorphosis target

**Prospective, authoritative for Genesis v2 direction. 11 September 2026.**

Genesis v1 is closed at its bounded DEVELOPMENT metamorphosis target. This document defines a new
research objective. It does not reinterpret the v1 milestone, move any frozen scientific gate, or
turn later work into evidence retroactively.

## Objective

> Build a persistent Genesis lineage that can **extend the language from which its own
> transformations are constructed**, empirically validate those extensions under the unchanged
> trust root, retain successful extensions as lineage-owned machinery, use them causally to create
> transformations that were not expressible before, survive process death, and repeat the process.

The key change from v1 is therefore not “more mutation kinds”. In v1, body programs, search policies
and MetaPolicies evolve, but the top-level language of policy mutation remains host-written. Genesis
v2 attacks that ceiling directly.

## Authority boundary that does not move

Open metamorphosis does **not** mean mutable evaluation authority. The following remain external to
the lineage and may not be made easier to change merely because v2 seeks a wider transformation
space:

- final accept/reject measure and trust-root source identity;
- evaluator/task grading contract;
- isolation boundary and forbidden authority;
- physical resource budgets and durable accounting;
- provenance rules and content-addressed identity;
- scientific gate definitions and frozen historical evidence.

A candidate language extension that can improve its score by modifying the measure is invalid by
construction, not an interesting descendant.

## The ladder

### V2-A — transformation operators become data

The host supplies only a small lower micro-step kernel. Named transformation operators are canonical
data interpreted by fixed apparatus. A lineage-held language can acquire a new operator definition
without adding a new Python mutation kind.

This stage is necessary but not sufficient: a host call that simply installs an authored operator is
not self-extension.

### V2-B — endogenous language extension

The lineage must diagnose that its current transformation language is insufficient, construct one or
more candidate operator definitions from the lower kernel, evaluate the resulting machinery on the
same objective under the unchanged trust root, and adopt a language descendant only when evidence
selects it.

The adopted operator must enable at least one policy/body transformation outside the complete image
of the predecessor language at the same admitted budget.

### V2-C — recursive extension

After the first extension has become ordinary held machinery, the lineage must later produce a
second transformation-language extension. The second extension must be causally dependent on the
first: ablation of the first extension must remove or materially reduce reach to the second under the
same budget and objective snapshot.

The launcher may supply objectives and fixed lower-level physical constraints. It may not select the
winning operator, choose the extension generation, or manually install the language descendant.

## Required runtime evidence

A successful v2 demonstration must record one continuous lineage in which all of the following are
mechanically checkable:

1. the predecessor transformation language is content-addressed and its complete admitted candidate
   image is measurable;
2. the lineage reaches an evidence-backed insufficiency/exhaustion state rather than receiving a
   host-authored “extend now” command;
3. candidate language extensions are lineage-produced canonical data from a prospectively admitted
   lower substrate;
4. candidates are evaluated under the same immutable trust root and evaluation contract;
5. rejection is retained as evidence and does not end the lineage;
6. an accepted extension is a direct content-addressed descendant of the held language;
7. the new language enables a transformation that the predecessor language could not express under
   the same bound;
8. that newly enabled transformation is actually used and causally matters to acquired work;
9. the lineage persists the language and resumes it after a fresh-process reconstruction;
10. a later language extension is produced and its reach is causally dependent on the earlier one;
11. the final lineage retains earlier capabilities while using the later transformation machinery;
12. the trust-root source identity, evaluation contract and forbidden-authority boundary remain
    unchanged throughout.

## Stopping criterion

Genesis v2 reaches this objective only when a canonical reproduction shows, without human
architectural intervention between stages, at least **two successive endogenous transformation-
language extensions** in one persistent lineage, with a real causal dependency from the second on
the first, followed by a transformation that was outside the predecessor language's complete
admitted image and that produces measured retained capability.

The reproduction must cross at least one fresh-process boundary after the first language extension
and must replay the completed campaign without redrawing already charged physical work.

## What success would not mean

Even a positive v2 DEVELOPMENT result would not by itself establish:

- AGI or human-level general intelligence;
- consciousness or agency in a philosophical sense;
- arbitrary code generation or unrestricted self-rewriting;
- arbitrary substrate invention;
- self-generated terminal goals;
- unbounded or indefinitely improving recursive self-improvement;
- permission to modify the evaluator, trust root, budgets, isolation or scientific rules;
- generality outside the admitted lower transformation substrate and tested objectives.

The intended defensible phrase, if the stopping criterion is eventually met, is narrower:

> Genesis demonstrates bounded recursive self-extension of its transformation language under an
> immutable external evaluation and authority boundary.

Until that canonical evidence exists, this document is an objective only.
