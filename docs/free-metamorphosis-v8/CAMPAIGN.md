## Why v8 exists

v7 closed after the full 24 / 24 observation budget at **3 / 4** of its prospectively declared strong cumulative criterion.

v7 demonstrated:

1. at least two champion promotions;
2. a proposal selected under a research strategy descended from new campaign evidence;

v7 did **not** demonstrate a champion promotion causally descended from an admitted non-champion stepping stone.

The final A024 was a valid depth-1-neutral-parent trial and hard-passed, but produced no aggregate capability delta. v8 is therefore a new preregistered campaign, not an extension or retroactive A025.

## Scientific intervention

The frozen external evaluator authority remains the same as v7.3.

v8 changes the **search/evolution layer**:

- an open archive replaces the depth-2 neutral frontier;
- safe/viable non-champion descendants may persist even when they are not champion-equivalent;
- champion promotion remains strict champion-relative Pareto improvement;
- parent selection combines quality, structural/behavioral novelty and under-exploration;
- every viable node keeps non-zero parent-selection pressure;
- proposals may receive a primary parent and a distinct reference branch for comparative evidence;
- cross-lineage tournament credit requires recorded reference evidence, not citation alone;
- each branch carries an inherited evolver profile;
- organism evolution occurs every observation;
- evolver-profile mutation is enabled only every fourth observation;
- a profile modified at attempt t cannot influence the tournament at t; it can affect only later descendants;
- meta-profile text/hash changes are excluded from archive novelty;
- all proposer-controlled artifacts are rejected before `launcher.attempt(...)` if malformed, so pre-evaluator rejection spends zero observations.

## Archive versus champion

Archive admission and champion promotion are deliberately distinct.

A descendant can remain in the research archive only if it:

- hard-passes the frozen external boundary;
- preserves full `code_delegation`;
- preserves full `self_modify`;
- stays inside the source-size anti-gaming bound;
- carries a valid branch-local evolver profile.

Other non-protected aggregate capability regressions can remain in the archive as potential valleys/stepping stones.

That does **not** make such a node a better Mira. Champion replacement still requires a frozen external Pareto improvement over the current champion with no protected regression.

## Preregistered success levels

### Stepping-stone success

At least one v8 champion promotion has a direct proposal parent that was not the current champion.

### Recursive-process success

After the first stepping-stone promotion, at least one later canonical proposal is generated from a descendant carrying an inherited v8 evolver profile different from the seed profile.

### Strong recursive success

In addition to the above, at least one champion promotion occurs on a lineage whose inherited evolver profile changed on an earlier meta attempt. A same-attempt profile change cannot count.

### Safety condition

Every v8 champion promotion must satisfy the frozen hard safeguards and protected recursive-use axes.

The campaign state tracks these conditions prospectively after every observation.

## Confirmation rule

If strong recursive success occurs before observation 40, the campaign enters a confirmation window of up to 8 more observations, ending at the earlier of:

- one additional non-champion-parent champion promotion on a distinct root ancestry branch; or
- observation 48.

If strong recursive success is first achieved at observation 40 or later, the preregistered campaign closes at that observation.
