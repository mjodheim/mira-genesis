# Genesis Free Metamorphosis v8 — publication boundary

The public v8 record may contain:

- preregistered protocol and stopping rules;
- aggregate capability-category outcomes after canonical observations;
- parent/child/tree/archive digests;
- public research-family/mechanism summaries;
- evolver-profile generation and content-addressed digest;
- control-plane identities and audit digests;
- external literature used to motivate the protocol.

The public record must not disclose:

- hidden evaluator case wording or authority copies;
- real secrets, credentials or connected-account data;
- private production telemetry;
- operational access paths;
- private organism source solely for the sake of disclosure;
- human attempt-specific guidance that would contaminate the proposer boundary.

External RSI literature is methodological context, not experimental evidence for Mira.

A proposal or control test is not a canonical capability observation. Only a run that enters the frozen external evaluator consumes the v8 observation budget.
