### M116

The DEVELOPMENT capability matrix established that the fixed route used by M116 enforced **none of
the nine structured-output schema feature classes** required by the frozen carrier schema despite a
catalogue capability declaration. The preregistered gate blocked the H61 freeze. M116 was closed
without an H61 qualifying generation or scientific observation.

### M117

M117 was explicitly instrument development and calibration. Its final apparatus selected the
OpenInference serving route for `deepseek/deepseek-v4-flash-0731` / canonical checkpoint
`deepseek/deepseek-v4-flash-20260731`. On the final DEVELOPMENT attempt, all twelve qualification
clauses passed and the route returned a census-dominating conforming stress completion of 68,368
tokens. Five apparatus revisions are disclosed, including revisions after endpoint observations;
therefore M117 does not claim the route-selection process was prospectively clean. H62 was never
frozen or tested.

### M118

M118 readiness calibration passed on that fixed route, including identity, all nine required schema
feature classes and a 73,731-token conforming stress completion. Two successive hostile reviews then
found Tier-1 defects in the inherited measurement and in its correction. M118 therefore stopped
before any H63 generation. The decision to simplify the instrument is itself an instrument-design
finding, not a scientific result.

### M119

M119 used the deliberately smaller instrument. The single qualifying request was delivered cleanly:
HTTP 200, one attempt, no retry, exact frozen model/provider identity, `finish_reason: stop`, parsed
completion and frozen output-schema conformance.

The generator emitted **37 machines for 36 requested**. After the single seal and authorized reveal:

- **3** were accepted by the frozen carrier host;
- **34** were refused by the frozen carrier host;
- **0** cleared the frozen qualification clauses;
- **0** paired demands were posed.

The frozen plan required at least three qualifying carriers and three distinct qualifying structures,
so the only valid verdict was `instrument_aborted`.

The principal refusal counts are instrument evidence:

- 25 carriers had an action argument domain outside 2–4;
- 8 carriers observed none of their own state;
- 1 carrier had a copy-source cell outside 0–1.

The first two expose the central M119 mismatch: **the frozen generator output schema permits values
that the frozen host must reject**. The model followed the schema; the instrument failed to make
schema conformance mechanically sufficient for host acceptance.

A second finding is equally important: **pre-seal admission established payload admissibility, not
scientific adequacy against the frozen plan minimum**. An admissible but inadequate bank could still
consume the one reveal.

## Frozen M119 checker defects

Closing review reproduced two real checker weaknesses after the freeze:

1. the checker trusted a caller-supplied analysis plan without re-deriving its commitment and
   thresholds from the committed design;
2. the replay gate authenticated the canonical measurements file but could score a different path
   supplied by the caller.

These defects **did not change the canonical M119 outcome**: the committed plan and committed
measurements were used, and the committed report replays byte-identically. They do narrow the claim
about checker robustness.

They are intentionally **not repaired in M119**. The checker is part of the tested-system freeze;
editing it after reveal would invalidate the chronology the freeze exists to protect.

## Next scientific frontier — prospective successor only

The next successor should aim to obtain an actual scientific verdict rather than another
instrument abort. Before any qualifying invocation is authorized, it should mechanically establish:

1. **Schema ↔ host equivalence for the relevant acceptance surface.** Anything the generator is
   allowed to emit must satisfy the frozen carrier host, or be transformed by a predeclared total,
   content-independent envelope whose adequacy can be proved before generation.
2. **Bank scientific-adequacy preflight before seal/reveal.** Admission must verify the frozen plan's
   minimum number of qualifying carriers and distinct structures, not merely payload syntax.
3. **Plan binding by re-derivation.** The checker must reconstruct or independently validate the
   frozen analysis plan and its decision thresholds rather than trust self-reported digest strings.
4. **Canonical evidence binding.** The checker must score only the committed canonical measurements
   artifact it authenticates; caller-selected measurement paths must not exist on the scientific
   entry point.
5. **Adversarial pre-freeze rehearsal of the complete success path.** Refusal-path tests are not
   evidence that result/replay paths work. The exact direct entry point must be exercised against a
   DEVELOPMENT result in a disposable checkout before freeze.
6. **No qualifying spend until all instrument gates pass.** A failing DEVELOPMENT gate is cheap and
   informative; a failing one-shot scientific generation closes a milestone.

`M120` / `H65` is a convenient proposed label for this successor, **not a registered fact** at this
snapshot. Registration, publication disposition, preregistration and freeze must occur in the proper
order before scientific execution.
