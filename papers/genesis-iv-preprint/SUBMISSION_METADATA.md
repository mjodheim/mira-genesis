## Short abstract

We report a bounded, prospectively validated two-transition process-improvement chain in Mira Genesis. V15.4 establishes G0->G1 real-organism process improvement because G1 retains the same champion quality while consuming two hidden-evaluator observations instead of four. After negative recursive attempts, V18 exposes a replay-overfitting shortcut that passes the public development surface but ties G1 on all 24 fresh holdout worlds. V19 prospectively expands development to 13 real and 188 synthetic replay worlds, forbids world-identity access, and selects an executable G2 before a new holdout exists. G2 then records 24 wins, 0 losses and 0 ties on the fresh holdout, improving mean replay core by 75.25 while reducing represented requests by 1.0417. In V20, G2 stops before hidden evaluation on the reconstructed A016 real-organism state; G1 requests two physical evaluations, both neutral at quality 953. Terminal utilities are G1=(0,0,-2) and G2=(0,0,0). Under the project's preregistered definition, this establishes bounded L3-P recursive process improvement across G0->G1->G2. It does not establish equal-budget recursive quality uplift, transfer, fully autonomous model-only self-authorship, or open-ended RSI.
