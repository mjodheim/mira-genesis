M111's ``diagnosis'' is also narrow. The policy detects underdetermination in a three-bit feature vocabulary and spends one authored probe. Calling it metacognitive is defensible only in this operational sense. It does not establish introspective access to arbitrary internal states or general uncertainty estimation.

\subsection{External validity}
The producer and consumer carriers are finite, synthetic, and project-authored. M110 is deliberately a stress test selected to realize row 5. The results may expose a general failure mode -- unsupported meta-rule extrapolation -- without establishing its prevalence in real agent systems.

M112 improves one aspect of external validity by removing direct project choice of world contents, but the carrier grammar, schema, evaluator, and selection criterion remain authored. The generator is small and may have unknown public pretraining exposure. Human-maintained sealed tasks and independent external domains remain absent.

\subsection{Statistical validity}
Most claims in M107--M111 are structural or unanimous over small frozen populations rather than population estimates. This is intentional: the experiments rely on exact counterfactuals, complete censuses, and deterministic evaluation. They should not be read as estimating broad frequencies of beneficial or harmful transfer.

M112's 20-world bank is especially small because of the preserved materialization defect. The 25\% ambiguous rate versus roughly 6\% in project-generated worlds is descriptive and not used for an inferential population claim. The scientifically relevant observations are the qualifying minima, reproduced outcome patterns, and the specific fixed-point counterexample.

\subsection{Reproducibility validity}
M107--M111 permit checker replay under the frozen runtime. M112 deliberately does not permit regenerating the bank because the one-shot/no-retry rule is part of the experiment. Its reproducibility therefore concerns public chronology, digests, preserved plaintext after reveal, isolation attestation, and deterministic recomputation over that bank. Readers who require independently regenerated data should treat M112 as weaker evidence and await an external bank.

\section{The next frontier: what Genesis III would have to remove}
