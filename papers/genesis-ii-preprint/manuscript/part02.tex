\paragraph{Carrier.} A carrier is the environment-specific structure in which the generic acquisition mechanism operates. Changing a carrier can change which feature rows are realizable while preserving the feature vocabulary and component registry. M110 changes the carrier from the producer's Boolean truth-table laboratory to reference-bearing JSON records over a four-valued chain. M112 changes world contents without removing the carrier contract itself.

\subsection{Freeze, preserve, check, replay}
Each canonical milestone separates four roles that are easy to collapse in ordinary experimental software:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Freeze:} the scientific question, apparatus, population or materialization procedure, decision predicates, runtime requirements, and relevant digests are fixed before canonical evidence is materialized.
  \item \textbf{Preserve:} the unique canonical result bytes are committed and tagged before the checker is invoked, so a failed checker cannot be followed by a silent rewrite of the result.
  \item \textbf{Check:} a separately implemented checker recomputes declared predicates from preserved evidence rather than trusting a runner's verdict field.
  \item \textbf{Replay:} where the protocol permits it, a stable evidence projection is recomputed in fresh processes and compared byte-for-byte after excluding declared ephemera such as PIDs and temporary paths.
\end{enumerate}
This is not third-party independence: the code and checker remain project-authored. It is a procedural barrier against changing the experiment after seeing the result.

M107--M111 use CPython 3.11.16 as the canonical frozen runtime. Their qualification executions record zero model, network, and remote-execution calls. M112 is intentionally different. The M110/M111 tested systems remain model-free, but a separate one-shot model invocation creates the held-out bank. Repeating that invocation would violate M112's no-retry rule and is therefore not a permitted reproduction operation.

\subsection{Causal identification rather than chronological storytelling}
Chronology alone would be weak evidence. The line therefore uses several recurring interventions:
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Ablation:} remove an acquired generation and require a byte-exact return to the predecessor state, then test that the claimed descendant capability disappears or the prior behavior returns.
  \item \textbf{Mutation:} change the semantic content of an acquired rule while preserving the surrounding mechanism, testing whether the result depends on the specific learned rule rather than mere registration.
  \item \textbf{Corruption:} damage content-addressed state and require a fail-closed identity check rather than silent fallback.
  \item \textbf{Fresh controls:} run predecessor states under the same world, demand, budget, and adapter bytes so a descendant is not compared with an easier experimental condition.
  \item \textbf{Structural certificates:} when possible, prove a target outside a held language by complete image enumeration or a monotonicity lemma rather than inferring impossibility from a shallow search failure.
  \item \textbf{Producer death and isolated consumers:} reconstruct from allowed state after the process that created it has terminated, excluding process-local memory as the explanation.
\end{itemize}
No single control proves causality in a philosophical sense. Together they target the main implementation-level alternative explanations: hidden host shortcuts, budget insufficiency, producer memory, stale fixture leakage, accidental state drift, and verdict fields that merely restate what the runner wrote.

\subsection{Negative evidence is admissible evidence}
The project records qualified negative results and checker-instrument failures instead of treating them as disposable debugging. That choice matters directly here. M110 is publishable because its harmful half is not treated as an embarrassment, and M112 is interpretable because a 22/24 inherited checker result remains negative rather than being relaxed after reveal. Earlier in the research line, M103 and M105 were permanently closed when their unique frozen checker invocations failed before completing the decision rule. Those failures are not scientific support for their hypotheses, but they demonstrate that a frozen instrument is allowed to invalidate an otherwise promising run.

\subsection{Claim boundary}
The paper makes no claim of AGI or general intelligence. It also makes no claim of intelligence explosion, open-ended self-improvement, autonomous objective formation, independent external reproduction, external human evaluation, or training-data independence of the M112 generator. The strongest evidence tier reached by M112 is a \code{blind\_generated\_sealed\_bank}: project context blindness and ordering are auditable, but human independence and third-party custody are not obtained.

The project's generality register requires all ten gates, independent reproduction, and external adversarial audit before a general-agent claim. None of M107--M112 closes a gate. The experiments below are mechanism evidence inside explicitly authored boundaries.
