\subsection{The harmful boundary can be derived before the consumer runs}
The negative half is not a post-hoc story fitted to row 5. Conservative adoption constrains an acquired rule only on rows the producer can reach. Rows 0, 4, and 5 lie outside that set. Rows 0 and 4 are excluded by a feature-semantics implication, $\neg g_1\Rightarrow g_2$, and cannot arise in any carrier implementing the same feature definitions. Row 5 is different. It is excluded in the producer only because prefix truncation makes $g_0\Rightarrow g_1$. That is a property of the producer family rather than a logical property of the vocabulary.

A carrier with a reference edge can break the implication and realize row 5. The adopted rule has a defined value there despite never having been constrained by evidence there. The producer record therefore predicts a region in which its conservatism guarantee is silent. The consumer experiment is still necessary: it establishes that such a row is actually realizable in the new carrier, that its limiting component differs, and that the extrapolation causes a wrong modification rather than remaining benign.
