\subsection{What blindness does and does not mean}
The generator is \code{qwen2.5:1.5b}, pinned by model blob, Ollama runtime version, and container image digest. Its sole input is a frozen text file plus constrained JSON schema. The model receives no repository mount and no network route. The isolation attestation is measured inside the container before invocation by inspecting interfaces, mount accessibility, traversal, symlinks, paths, and environment variables. The invocation terminates normally with \code{done\_reason=stop}, produces 5,488 tokens and 100 records, and is the only qualifying generation call.

This establishes \emph{context blindness} under the measured execution boundary: the generator cannot read the repository context used to design the experiment. It does not establish that the model's pretraining data never contained public information about \genesis or related concepts. Training-data independence is explicitly not obtained. Nor is human independence obtained: the project authored the schema and prompt, selected the pinned model before generation, and controls the seal and reveal key.

\subsection{A frozen materialization defect that was not repaired}
The frozen specification assigned the requested world count to the record count even though five records form one world. The single invocation therefore produced 100 records, materializing 20 worlds instead of the intended 100. The defect was discovered and recorded after the invocation but before sealing or reveal. Repairing it would have required a second qualifying generation call after learning something about the first output, violating the frozen no-retry rule. The defect was left in place.

This is a meaningful loss of statistical breadth, not a cosmetic bug. The pre-seal record noted that the smaller bank substantially reduced the expected probability of meeting the planned minimum counts. The revealed bank nevertheless contained five ambiguous worlds and six witness worlds, above the precommitted minima of three each, plus nine worlds in neither stratum. All twenty were subjected to the public classification rule; no post-reveal world selection occurred.

The blind distribution also differs visibly from the project-generated one. The ambiguous rate is 25\% in the 20-world blind bank versus roughly 6\% across 1,160 project-generated worlds. With such a small bank, this is not presented as a population estimate. Its importance is qualitative: the generator produces a distribution that is not simply the project's hand-authored sampling routine replayed through another interface.

\subsection{Blind transfer: scientific outcomes reproduce, checker stays negative}
The frozen M110 apparatus is applied unmodified to the six qualifying blind transfer worlds. Every scientific outcome in Table~\ref{tab:transfer} reproduces: row 7 is gained by generation one; row 3 is additionally gained by generation two; row 1 remains conserved; and the harmful row-5 transfer remains \mzero 6/6 versus \mone/\mtwo 0/6. \reach remains strict on every world.

The inherited M110 checker nevertheless returns 22/24. Two predicates are false and neither is relaxed:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{P1 is an invocation artifact.} M110's original preflight requires a population tagged \code{canonical} at the canonical M110 path. The revealed M112 bank is intentionally evaluated from a distinct scratch/reveal path. This failure is reported because the inherited checker is not edited to accommodate the successor experiment.
  \item \textbf{P5 is a real measurement.} On one blind world, the constructive image has size 17 at node bound 7 and 18 at bounds 9, 11, and 13. M110's fixed-point certificate required equality at all four bounds. The operating bound is 9 and the image is stable from 9 onward, so the posed reach outcomes do not change, but the certificate is false.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Blind diagnosis}
The frozen M111 apparatus computes 24/24 true on the blind bank. Across five ambiguous worlds and both probe orders, \mzero and \mone solve neither member of the pair, \mtwo solves only $A$, \code{always\_signal} solves only $B$, never-probe and always-probe solve only $A$, and the acquired diagnostic policy solves both 5/5. The pooled record now contains 44 episodes from 11 worlds; the undetermined row remains 3 and the determined rows remain $\{1,5,7\}$. Generation-three acquisition again occurs in the 127-program space with seven consistent policies, and generation-two ablation again refuses.

M112 therefore supports a narrower statement than independent reproduction: the M111 diagnostic phenomenon and the scientific outcome pattern of M110 do not depend on the project having directly selected the world contents. The experiment does not remove carrier authorship, evaluator authorship, feature-vocabulary authorship, registry authorship, probe authorship, possible training-data contamination, or project custody.
