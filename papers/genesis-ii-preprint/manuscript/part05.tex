\subsection{A materially different carrier}
The producer laboratory operates over three-signal Boolean structures. The consumer operates over five-document reference-bearing JSON worlds whose values lie on the chain $0<1<2<3$. A reference edge breaks one structural implication that was guaranteed by the producer's prefix-truncation family. The registry and feature vocabulary are deliberately shared so the transferred object is the attribution machinery rather than a new representation designed for the consumer.

The producer reaches rows $\{1,2,3,6,7\}$. The consumer reaches $\{1,3,5,7\}$. Labels agree on every shared row. Row 5 is the critical novelty: it is realized by the consumer and by no producer state. Thus the experiment exposes an extrapolation region without changing the rule representation or the component names.

\subsection{The harmful boundary can be derived before the consumer runs}
The negative half is not a post-hoc story fitted to row 5. Conservative adoption constrains an acquired rule only on rows the producer can reach. Rows 0, 4, and 5 lie outside that set. Rows 0 and 4 are excluded by a feature-semantics implication, $\neg g_1\Rightarrow g_2$, and cannot arise in any carrier implementing the same feature definitions. Row 5 is different. It is excluded in the producer only because prefix truncation makes $g_0\Rightarrow g_1$. That is a property of the producer family rather than a logical property of the vocabulary.

A carrier with a reference edge can break the implication and realize row 5. The adopted rule has a defined value there despite never having been constrained by evidence there. The producer record therefore predicts a region in which its conservatism guarantee is silent. The consumer experiment is still necessary: it establishes that such a row is actually realizable in the new carrier, that its limiting component differs, and that the extrapolation causes a wrong modification rather than remaining benign.

This distinction matters for self-improving systems. Enlarging a search space, action repertoire, or modification language can be a real capacity gain while worsening the policy that chooses among those possibilities. The result therefore argues against using a monotone capacity metric as a surrogate for realized competence under distribution shift.

\subsection{Alternative explanations and controls}
M110 includes several controls aimed at narrower explanations:
\begin{itemize}
  \item a deeper node bound of 13 leaves the fresh predecessor failures at rows 7 and 3 unchanged, so those are not shallow search failures;
  \item monotone-closure and visible-function certificates establish structural boundaries of the held spaces;
  \item fixed-point checks run at bounds 7, 9, 11, and 13 on every canonical world;
  \item removing generation two returns byte-exactly to \mone and loses row 3;
  \item removing generation one returns byte-exactly to \mzero, loses row 7, and regains row 5;
  \item a semantic mutation of generation two loses row 3;
  \item a built-but-unregistered rule leaves the state without that acquisition and does not obtain the claimed behavior;
  \item identity corruption fails closed;
  \item all compared arms share world, demand, adapter, and budget bytes and differ in the rule cascade;
  \item host-widened shortcuts show that the host can directly widen the named components, clarifying that the claim is about attribution and state-held machinery rather than a capability the host itself lacks.
\end{itemize}
All P1--P24 compute true with replay equality across 186 isolated processes and zero external calls. The consumer family remains project-authored and was deliberately chosen to realize the stress row. M110 is therefore a designed causal stress test, not independent-domain transfer.

\section{M111: self-directed diagnosis under a scarce experiment budget}
\label{sec:m111}
M110 shows a failure of extrapolation: the inherited feature row can drive a confident but wrong attribution outside the producer geometry. M111 asks a more precise successor question. Can the lineage use its own accumulated record to identify observations for which the feature vocabulary does not determine the answer, and can it spend a limited experiment only there?
