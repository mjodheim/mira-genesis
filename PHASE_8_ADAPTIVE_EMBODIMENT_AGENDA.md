# Phase 8 — adaptive embodiment agenda

M066 confirmed the bounded Genesis construction objective and closed the M043–M066 line. Phase 8
does not reopen that result by adding budgets or tasks. It asks a materially different question:

> Can a continuing lineage discover the execution contract of a body whose complete adapter was
> not supplied, preserve inherited behaviour through that contract and refuse to adapt when the
> available evidence is insufficient or inconsistent?

The phase concerns **adaptive embodiment**: changing how a preserved competence is physically
encoded, invoked and read back. It is not a claim of arbitrary hardware support.

## Entry experiment — M067

M067 removes the complete target adapter that remained authored in M066. Four precommitted bodies
are exposed only through opaque handles in a separate process. The discoverer receives accepted or
rejected byte transactions and raw four-byte replies. It must infer, from public observations of
its inherited source body:

- one of three frame families;
- one of two checksum rules;
- the permutation between four opaque operation bytes and four inherited skills;
- reply offset, byte order and value transform.

The candidate grammar, observation domains, result scale and attempt cap are frozen. Every public
survivor is validated on a disjoint hidden domain before deterministic selection. The same search
must work for the entire committed bank; no favourable body may be chosen after observation.

## Required falsifiers

An experiment in this phase stops without a positive verdict when any of these occurs:

1. a complete descriptor or target adapter crosses the opaque boundary;
2. hidden evidence is reachable by the discovery API;
3. any public survivor is removed by digest choice before independent hidden validation;
4. a default adapter or framing-only control solves the body without semantic discovery;
5. an empty or corrupted transcript still yields an adapter;
6. the source target values are asserted rather than observed from the inherited lineage;
7. repository, network, credential or deployment authority is granted to the lineage.

## Claim ladder

M067 can support only **bounded opaque-contract discovery across a committed body class**. A later
successor would need a fresh falsifiable question, for example learning a descriptor grammar not
fixed in advance or crossing a real device boundary. Merely adding more bodies, operations or
candidate combinations does not count as a phase advance.

The phase remains separate from M045's measurement track. Better embodiment does not validate a
proxy objective, and proxy performance cannot establish successful re-embodiment.
