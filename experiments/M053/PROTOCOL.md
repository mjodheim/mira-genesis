# M053 — bounded endogenous transformation-language extension

**Status: PROPOSED — unqualified.**

## Research question

Can the continuing lineage prove that the frozen 80-program founder language is insufficient for a public task, synthesize a concrete reusable transformation only after observing that limitation, pass independent hidden validation, adopt it transactionally, reuse it on a structurally distinct reduction family, preserve a negative episode, and roll back exactly?

## Bounded construction

The founder language is the unchanged M051 catalogue of 80 programs. The extension mechanism is a safe, closed pair-expression meta-language with exactly 16 ASTs: two operands (`current`, `previous`), four binary operators (`add`, `subtract`, `minimum`, `maximum`) and two operand positions. No concrete pair transformation is accepted in the founder registry.

The public creation task uses a sum reduction over adjacent transitions. Before synthesis, every founder candidate is exhaustively evaluated against all public probes; synthesis is forbidden unless zero founder candidates survive. The proposer then exhaustively evaluates the 16 meta-programs using public probes only. A unique survivor may be submitted to an independent hidden validator.

The positive target is the post-limitation expression `current subtract previous`. It is not stored as an accepted founder primitive. Its artifact is content-addressed and adopted only after hidden validation.

## Reuse and negative evidence

The adopted extension must subsequently solve a distinct maximum-over-adjacent-transitions task that no founder candidate solves on the reuse probe. Direct replay of the creation answer cannot satisfy this second reduction family.

A deliberately weak public episode must remain ambiguous and terminate as `insufficient_evidence`; the search budget and language are not widened after that verdict. A contradictory hidden probe must return a negative validation result.

## Transaction and rollback

The founder and adopted registries have deterministic content-addressed checkpoints. An unvalidated artifact cannot be adopted. A forced post-adoption fault is represented by discarding the proposed registry and restoring the exact founder registry; the checkpoint must match byte-for-byte.

## Qualification rule

M053 may pass in development only when the complete Python 3.11 and Python 3.13 matrices and repository-integrity job pass on the exact documented head. Every failed run must remain in append-only qualification history. No merge is permitted while any required job fails or remains unresolved.

## Anti-cheating and claim boundary

The concrete accepted expression is synthesized after founder insufficiency is demonstrated. Hidden probes are unavailable to synthesis. The artifact is not a lookup table, and the second task changes the reduction family. The proposer has no validation or repository authority.

This is still a tiny finite meta-language and one bounded experiment. It does not establish arbitrary code generation, unrestricted self-modification, open-ended evolution, unknown-runtime discovery, general intelligence, consciousness or production safety. Network, repository, credential, deployment and external-system authority remain human-controlled. M053 is noncanonical; M042 remains the only positive canonical continuous-lineage completion.
