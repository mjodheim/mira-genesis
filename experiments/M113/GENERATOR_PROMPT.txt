Emit a JSON object with a single key "machines" whose value is a list of exactly N entries.

Each entry describes one small machine, and is an object with exactly six keys.

"surface" : an object with exactly eight keys describing how the machine is spoken to.
    "kind"            : one of "json_object", "text_line", "packed_digits", "json_array".
    "ok_token"        : a lowercase ASCII word of 2 to 8 characters, letters and digits only,
                        beginning with a letter.
    "error_token"     : another such word, different from "ok_token".
    "field_separator" : one of " ", ":", ",", ";", "|".
    "pair_separator"  : one of "=", "-", "/".
    "action_key"      : a lowercase ASCII name of 2 to 12 characters, letters, digits and
                        underscores, beginning with a letter.
    "argument_key"    : another such name, different from the other two keys.
    "status_key"      : another such name, different from the other two keys.

"cells" : a list of 1 to 4 objects, each with exactly two keys.
    "name" : a lowercase ASCII name of 2 to 12 characters, letters, digits and underscores,
             beginning with a letter. No two cells share a name, and no cell shares a name with
             "action_key", "argument_key" or "status_key".
    "size" : an integer from 2 to 4. The cell holds an integer from 0 to size minus one.

"initial" : a list of integers, exactly as long as "cells". Entry i is from 0 to the size of cell
            i minus one.

"visible" : a list of booleans, exactly as long as "cells". At least one entry is true.

"errors" : a list of 1 to 4 distinct lowercase ASCII names, each 2 to 12 characters, letters,
           digits and underscores, beginning with a letter.

"actions" : a list of 2 to 6 objects, each with exactly six keys.
    "name"     : a lowercase ASCII name as above. No two actions share a name.
    "arity"    : 0 or 1.
    "arg_size" : an integer from 2 to 4 when "arity" is 1, and 0 when "arity" is 0.
    "guard"    : a list of 0 to 3 objects, each with exactly three keys.
                 "cell"     : an integer index into "cells".
                 "relation" : one of "eq", "ne", "lt", "ge".
                 "value"    : an integer from 0 to the size of that cell minus one.
    "effect"   : a list of 1 to 3 objects, each with exactly three keys.
                 "cell"    : an integer index into "cells".
                 "mode"    : one of "set", "add", "sub", "arg", "copy". Use "arg" only when
                             "arity" is 1.
                 "operand" : an integer. For "copy" it is an index into "cells". For "arg" it is 0.
                             Otherwise it is from 0 to the size of that cell minus one.
    "error"    : one of the names in "errors".

Produce the entries independently of one another. Do not repeat an entry, and do not produce two
entries that differ only in the names, tokens or separators they use. Vary the surface kind, the
number of cells, their sizes, which of them are visible, the number of actions, their arities,
their guards and their effects across the list.

Do not add keys, comments, prose, explanation or any other text: the response must be the JSON
object and nothing else.

You are not told what these machines are for, and you should not guess. Do not attempt to make them
interesting, balanced, adversarial, representative, easy or difficult. Any such attempt would make
the output worse for its purpose.
