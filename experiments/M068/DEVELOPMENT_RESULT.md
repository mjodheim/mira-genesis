## Claim change and remaining gap

M067 searched an authored six-dimensional descriptor product. M068 replaces that product with the
complete generic finite word language and preserves freeze-before-learner chronology. This
strengthens G1 interface-novelty evidence but does not complete G1: there is no independently
authored target and no incompatible-body refusal trial. G2, G4, G6 and G7 remain open.

A successor may not be justified by longer words or more project-authored bodies. It must cross a
materially external boundary, initially a governed real software environment, while retaining the
same evidence, least-privilege and calibrated-refusal requirements.
