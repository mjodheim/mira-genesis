# M078 calibrated refusal on an incompatible opaque body

**FROZEN BEFORE IMPLEMENTING OR MATERIALIZING THE DISCOVERER AND BODY BANK.**

## The exact clause this addresses

G1 reads, in full: the agent must enter bodies whose interaction language was not encoded as a
descriptor product in the discoverer; targets are frozen before the learner, selected after freeze
and unavailable for source inspection; and **at least one incompatible body must produce a
calibrated refusal rather than an invented adapter**.

M068 satisfied the first parts. It froze four opaque command languages before the learner, scanned
the complete 37,448-word space and induced four distinct adapters, each passing 12/12 hidden
observations. Every body in that bank was solvable. The refusal clause has never been tested.

## Why a naive version of this experiment would be worthless

If an incompatible body simply had no candidate at all, refusing would reduce to reporting an empty
search. That measures nothing: any procedure that returns its best survivor already returns nothing
when there are no survivors.

Each incompatible body here therefore **admits at least one candidate that reproduces every public
observation**. Two inherited skills are mapped onto body commands whose replies are identical for
every word in the bounded language. A procedure that adopts the best public fit will find one, adopt
it, and be wrong — the arbitrary choice between two collapsed skills fails hidden validation.

Refusal thus requires recognising that the evidence is **under-determined**, not that it is absent.
That is the difference between a calibrated refusal and an exhausted search, and the protocol treats
refusal caused by an empty candidate set as a non-result.

## What the discoverer may see

Public probing only. The aliased skill pair is discoverable from public observations: two skills
that the inherited source body distinguishes produce identical replies for every word the opaque
body accepts. The discoverer can establish that by probing, so its refusal is grounded in something
it observed rather than something it was told.

It never receives the body class, the hidden observations, the aliased pair, or any descriptor or
complete adapter across the opaque boundary. Candidate code does not execute in the process holding
hidden cases — the falsifier that post-hoc disqualified M069.

## Falsifiable claim

One unchanged procedure over a bank of four compatible and four incompatible bodies must:

- recover a correct adapter for all four compatible bodies, each passing 12/12 hidden observations;
- emit **zero** false refusals on compatible bodies;
- refuse all four incompatible bodies with **zero** invented adapters.

Two matched controls fix the ends of the scale. `always_refuse` must recover no adapter at all, so
refusal cannot be earned by refusing everything. `never_refuse` must emit a public-fitting adapter on
every incompatible body and fail hidden validation, which is what demonstrates that the public
evidence really is insufficient and that the discoverer's refusal was informative.

Any missed compatible adapter, any false refusal, or any accepted incompatible body makes M078
negative. If an incompatible body turns out to admit a genuinely correct adapter, the result is
inconclusive rather than negative: that would mean the construction is wrong, not the discoverer.

## Relationship to M074

M074 found that an external model, given three capability-absent tasks, refused none of them. M078
is not a repair of that result and cannot be cited as one. Its discoverer is deterministic and
model-free, so a positive outcome shows that a bounded search procedure can be built to detect
observable under-determination — not that any agent possesses epistemic humility. The two results
live on different tracks and neither transfers to the other.

## Safety boundary

The discoverer receives compute and in-process memory only. Bodies are in-process opaque handles
behind a byte interface. No arm may reach a network, repository write path, credential, deployment
path, permission interface or physical actuator.

## Claim boundary

A positive result supplies the missing G1 refusal clause as **bounded mechanism evidence** on a
project-authored bank. It does **not** close G1: closing it requires bodies whose interaction
language is maintained outside this project and independent reproduction. It establishes no
cross-domain transfer, no general epistemic humility, no Genesis Gate 2 evidence and no AGI claim.
