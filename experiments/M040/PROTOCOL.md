# M040 — canonical cumulative post-migration plasticity

**Status: frozen pre-result protocol. The sealed task seed does not exist until the exact
marker-only arming commit is immutable.**

## Canonical question

Can one deterministic lineage accumulate exact improvements and a lineage-owned
transformation tool in substrate A, discover and migrate to an initially opaque substrate B,
then use both its transported tool registry and causal continuation state to diagnose and
complete another exact self-rewrite on a task revealed only after migration?

The claim is bounded to the binary-DFA laboratory and the exact mechanism frozen with this
protocol. It is not a claim of arbitrary program synthesis, unrestricted self-modification,
open-ended evolution, general intelligence or consciousness.

## Frozen ancestry

M040 composes, without modifying their canonical artefacts:

- M039's three-cycle cumulative lineage, lineage-owned tool provenance, exact rollback and
  seed-only replay;
- M013e/M032's opaque-substrate discovery, native synthesis and canonical transport boundary;
- M038's exact structural-incapacity certificate;
- the M040 packet, lineage-anchor generator, six-arm comparison, independent search audits
  and persisted-result verifier present in the frozen parent commit.

The frozen mechanism is the complete repository state at the parent of the marker-only
arming commit. The protocol commitment used by the engine is `sha256:<protocol SHA-256>`.

## One continuous lineage

From the sealed M040 task seed, the complete arm must:

1. derive and complete a fresh three-cycle M039-style lineage in substrate A;
2. retain the exact final source DFA, full serialised registry and causal continuation state;
3. receive an opaque Boolean machine whose opcode semantics were unavailable before the
   migration boundary;
4. discover those semantics only through the public probe interface;
5. synthesise an exact native body for the final pre-migration DFA;
6. commit and rehydrate a canonical packet containing the source representation, native body,
   discovered substrate, registry and learning state;
7. derive the post-migration task only after packet validation;
8. diagnose structural incapacity from the committed oracle observations;
9. derive lineage anchors only from transported pre-migration continuation programs and tool
   provenance;
10. search for an exact rewrite under the frozen equal budget;
11. synthesise the accepted rewrite exactly on substrate B;
12. reject one fixed bad provisional source/native pair and restore the accepted pair exactly;
13. reproduce packet bytes, journal bytes, all pre- and post-migration searches, controls,
    audits and decisions from the seed and immutable inputs.

## Frozen lineage-anchor task family

After packet validation, the generator:

1. derives every unique contiguous one- or two-tool fragment of a transported continuation
   program that preserves tool order and contains at least one `lineage_constructed` tool;
2. derives every one- or two-tool suffix over the protocol-supplied primitive registry;
3. orders anchors and suffixes deterministically from the post-migration task seed;
4. considers at most 2,048 combined programs of total symbolic length at most four;
5. accepts the first program that:
   - produces a strictly larger minimal DFA than the migrated parent;
   - is not reached by birth-registry enumeration within depth four and 4,096 nodes;
   - yields a sound exact structural-incacity certificate from the committed observations.

If no admissible task exists within the bound, the canonical scientific outcome is negative.
The generator may not increase its bound, deepen search, add another suffix or use target-aware
fallback on the same sealed seed.

The selected suffix, target, target digest and successful candidate are absent from the
transport packet.

## Learning state

The packet carries only information derived before migration:

- accepted pre-migration candidate identifiers;
- lineage-owned tool identifiers and causal uses;
- preferred tool order;
- adopted continuation programs;
- the declared exploration depth and remaining node budget.

It may not carry the post-migration target, selected suffix, successful candidate or any
evaluator-only target information.

The complete arm derives and evaluates lineage-anchor adaptations before generic search.
The learning-state ablation receives the same migrated body, full registry, observations,
depth and node budget, but no continuation frontier.

## Frozen arms

Every arm receives the same 127 observations, symbolic depth four, exact evaluator and equal
4,096-node symbolic-search budget.

- **A — complete migrated lineage:** migrated parent, full registry and full learning state.
- **B — fresh on B:** migrated parent and birth primitives, no lineage-owned tool or memory.
- **C — unchanged parent migrated:** original founder migrated to B, birth registry and empty
  learning state.
- **D — output only:** exact migrated native behaviour but no portable source, registry or
  rewrite state.
- **E — learning-state ablation:** migrated parent and full registry, but no continuation
  frontier or preferred ordering.
- **F — learned-tool ablation:** migrated parent and sanitised learning state with every
  lineage-owned tool removed.

A deterministic counter value of 4,097 records the first over-budget expansion.

## Frozen budgets

- pre-migration mechanism: frozen M039 constants;
- observation depth: 6, yielding 127 binary words;
- substrate probe budget: 120;
- migration candidate evaluations: 75,000;
- native component budget: 320;
- serialised native-byte budget: 16,777,216;
- post-migration symbolic depth: 4;
- equal symbolic-search budget per arm: 4,096 nodes;
- lineage-anchor task-generation bound: 2,048 combined programs;
- forced rollback attempts: exactly one.

The 4,096-node bound is explicitly calibration-derived from consumed development seed 400044.
It was committed before the first valid lineage-anchor development seed 400047 and is not
presented as an untouched prediction. Seeds 400040 through 400047 are consumed development
history and may not be used for the canonical task.

No arm receives a hidden fallback, a larger budget or a deeper search.

## Primary verdicts

### Trans-substrate continuity supported

All of the following are required:

- three exact pre-migration improvement cycles complete;
- the final pre-migration body migrates exactly;
- packet serialisation round-trips canonically and rejects tampering;
- the task is derived only after migration and packet validation;
- the complete arm adopts an exact post-migration source body;
- the accepted native body is exactly equivalent to that source body and target;
- the fixed failed provisional adoption rolls back exactly;
- seed-only replay reproduces every committed identity and transcript.

### Post-migration plasticity supported

Continuity must be supported, and:

- arm A reaches exact quality 127/127 within budget;
- arms B, C, D, E and F remain non-exact under the same committed opportunity;
- B, C, E and F terminate at the deterministic 4,097th over-budget expansion;
- A uses strictly fewer symbolic nodes than every search-capable control;
- A's accepted tool sequence equals the hidden task-generating sequence;
- that sequence contains a lineage-owned tool created before migration;
- native synthesis, rollback, independent audits and seed-only replay are exact.

This is deliberately stricter than the early development draft: the canonical persisted-result
verifier requires the learning-state ablation to remain non-exact as well.

A positive continuity result with negative plasticity is a valid negative plasticity result.
A task-generation or mechanism-domain failure is also preserved as the first canonical
scientific outcome.

## Independent verification

For a positive result, `metamorphosis.m040_result_verify.verify_m040_result` must independently
recalculate and accept:

- the exact six-arm set and all arm counters;
- all three pre-migration search audits;
- all six post-migration search audits;
- equal budgets and deterministic exhaustion;
- the complete twenty-record M040 causal hash chain and event order;
- packet, task, migration, certificate, native-control, adoption and rollback
  cross-references.

The operational engine's summary is not sufficient evidence on its own.

## Replay boundary

Replay may receive only:

- the sealed master seed derived from the immutable arming head;
- this protocol's SHA-256 commitment;
- the frozen parent commit;
- expected external artefact identities used only for comparison.

It may not receive a target DFA, accepted candidate, generated program, discovered truth
tables, migrated body, control outcome or mutation trace from the expected result.

## Canonical execution integrity

1. This protocol, the mechanism, runner, sealed derivation, guard, verifier and workflow are
   committed before any canonical task seed is derived.
2. The freeze-candidate workflow computes identities only and confirms the marker is absent.
3. The canonical block opens only on one commit that changes exactly
   `experiments/M040/CANONICAL_ARMED.json` and carries the exact message
   `m040(canonical): arm first immutable run`.
4. The marker binds the actual parent commit and exact protocol SHA-256.
5. The sealed seed is derived from the immutable arming-head SHA and protocol digest.
6. The first execution is preserved whether positive or negative.
7. No rerun may replace the first artefact, and no threshold, budget, generator or rule may be
   changed after observing it.
8. Later executions, if ever permitted for reproduction, are reproductions only and cannot
   alter the scientific record.

## Falsifiers

The corresponding verdict is rejected if any of the following occurs:

1. task information is observed before packet validation;
2. substrate semantics are supplied rather than discovered;
3. migration or native synthesis is not exact;
4. packet tampering, omission or reordering is not detected;
5. the incapacity certificate cannot be independently recomputed;
6. the accepted rewrite uses no pre-migration lineage-owned tool;
7. any required control solves exactly under the same budget;
8. output-only proposes a rewrite;
9. rollback fails to restore the exact accepted source/native pair;
10. replay consumes an expected target, candidate, substrate or mutation trace;
11. any audit, packet, journal, body, task, control or transcript digest changes;
12. an arm receives a different depth, observation set or budget;
13. the lineage-anchor generator exceeds 2,048 programs or falls back;
14. any frozen rule changes after the first canonical outcome is observed.
