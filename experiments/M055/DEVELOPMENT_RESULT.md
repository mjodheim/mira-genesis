# M055 — development result

## Status

**NEGATIVE ON ITS CENTRAL CLAIM.**

The construction ran as specified. The acquisition it produced does not confer a capability the
same budget could not reach without it. This is the result that closed the language-extension
line under **D019**.

## What holds

Within the frozen M055 protocol and test bank:

- the lineage begins from the accepted M048 **version-eight** native state, reconstructed rather
  than asserted, carrying **32** retained capabilities and ten native modules including
  `tool_mean` learned before the first migration and `tool_max` learned after it;
- it diagnoses the unknown token `variation` in the migrated body;
- it **constructs** `maximum(current - previous, previous - current)` at formation depth two,
  touching **722** candidates out of an admissible **29,330,422** — below even the depth-two
  space of 2,422, so enumeration was impossible rather than merely avoided;
- the constructed tool is compiled to JavaScript and executed in the migrated runtime; the
  emitted module carries executable semantics;
- **all 32 inherited capabilities are executed and pass** in the candidate body before adoption.
  Requirement 7 of issue #77 is measured, not assumed;
- adoption reaches version nine; the reuse construction re-verifies **37** inherited capabilities;
- the acquired expression is used as material by the next construction —
  `multiply(ACQUIRED, ACQUIRED)`;
- ambiguous public evidence terminates without commitment;
- a forced post-adoption fault is detected, the intact state reports no fault under the same
  detector, and the exact state is restored byte for byte;
- the manifest is deterministic and reproducible across processes;
- repository, network, credential, deployment and production authority remain absent.

This is the only experiment in the M053–M055 line that satisfies requirement 1 of issue #72:
continuing the qualified M048 lineage rather than starting from an empty registry.

## What does not hold

The ablation gives the from-scratch arm the same composition power as the continued lineage and
the same budget. **It solves the reuse task anyway.**

| Arm | Candidates constructed | Solves |
|---|---:|---|
| Continued lineage, acquired expression available | 48 | yes |
| Empty acquisition, same power, same budget | 737 | **yes** |

The acquisition made the search roughly fifteen times cheaper and made nothing newly reachable.
A search-efficiency gain over a fixed language is what M052 was, and what D016 closed. M055 as
designed therefore reproduces the pattern the issue behind it was written to escape.

## Two refuted designs, and why there is no third

Two reuse tasks were tried.

1. **Maximum of second-order absolute differences.** Refuted because the mechanism applied a
   candidate twice for every arm, so repeated application was not something the acquisition
   provided.
2. **Sum of `max(|d|, |d|·|d|)` per adjacent pair.** Refuted because that expression reduces to
   `|d|·|d|`, reachable at formation depth two. The reasoning that placed it at depth four was
   wrong.

Two designs, one structural cause. A third was not attempted.

Choosing the task that flatters the hypothesis after seeing two ablations is the tuning illusion
recorded in CHANGELOG 0.33.0, where a swept configuration scored 9/12 on the cases being watched
and 3/12 on untouched ones. A capability gain can also be manufactured here by lowering the
depth bound or the budget until the control fails, but then the gain is an artifact of the
declared bound and the experiment measures its own instrument.

## Why the negative is structural

In a language closed under composition, with a budget that reaches the relevant depth, acquiring
a sub-expression cannot enlarge what is expressible. Everything the acquisition makes reachable
was already reachable by rebuilding it. The acquisition is a cache.

That argument is recorded as **D019**, which closes the line and states that M053, M054 and M055
may not be cited as evidence that the lineage grew a capability.

## What this experiment found on the way

M055 could not publish its inherited state identity when it was written. M048 computed
`validation_digest` over a selection mapping containing the Node worker pid, so the identity
drifted between processes and carried that drift into the patch registry, the native journal and
the causal memory.

That defect was found here, repaired under **D018**, and the identity is publishable again. The
repair moved two M048 manifest fields and changed no finding.

## Claim boundary

One bounded lineage, one formation language, fixed task families, one runtime. M055 does not
establish arbitrary code generation, unrestricted self-modification, open-ended evolution,
unknown-runtime discovery, general intelligence, consciousness or production safety.

M055 is noncanonical. **M042 remains the only positive canonical continuous-lineage
completion.**

## Where the frontier went

To repeated trans-substrate continuity — issue #80. A substrate is not closed under composition,
so the argument that closed this line does not apply to it. M048 changed substrate once, with a
hand-written compiler, on a protocol that told it when to move. Nothing has migrated twice, and
whether a capability learned after a migration survives the next one has never been asked.
