## H51

H51 is H50's question asked again on a fresh population with a corrected instrument.

Starting from the exact positive M104 V3 bytes, an initially empty state-owned constructor-feature
registry can acquire, independently validate and register a carrier-neutral executable Boolean
classifier F from DEVELOPMENT-only behavior without receiving F's body, identifier, digest or
future consumer material. F must survive producer death and enable later generalized acquisitions
in JSON-document and SQLite carriers whose definitions are outside the complete finite exact-context
dispatch image of the frozen M104 constructor. An otherwise identical fresh lineage with the same
M104 predecessor, runtime, lower substrate, later observations and complete semantic search, but no
F bytes and no DEVELOPMENT material, must refuse or fail to validate those acquisitions for a
structural/epistemic reason rather than search exhaustion. F must remain a live content-addressed
dependency, all M104 and predecessor capabilities must remain conserved, and corruption plus exact
rollback must behave as predicted under stable replay.

A positive result would establish **bounded state-owned constructor-vocabulary extension**. It would
not establish endogenous ownership of the lower interpreter, open-ended vocabulary growth,
self-hosting, recursive self-improvement, independent task authorship, a closed generality gate,
general-agent evidence or AGI.

therefore binds the mechanism module **unchanged**:

- `metamorphosis/m105_runtime.py` is imported, not copied, adapted, forked or edited. Its raw

- Only three things may change: the qualification population, the DEVELOPMENT fixture, and the

Any change to the mechanism module invalidates M106 as a replication and requires a distinct
hypothesis.

## Instrument correction — the defect that lost M105

M105 died because `check_m105_result.py` deferred `from scripts import run_m105_qualification` into
its `--replay` branch with no `sys.path` bootstrap, and the pre-freeze verification exercised only
the refusal path, which returns before that import.

M106 may not freeze until the following is demonstrated in a throwaway clean checkout, on the
canonical runtime, using **exactly** the commands and entry points the canonical chronology will
use:

1. a complete DEVELOPMENT result is materialized through the real canonical code path;
2. the checker runs against it as a **direct script**, through the **replay branch**, and computes
   every predicate;
3. exit codes are asserted for result-present, result-absent, corrupted-result and negative-verdict
   cases;
4. the check report artifact is produced and its digest verified;
5. every deferred import in the checker resolves under the exact frozen command;
6. the rehearsal consumes no canonical attempt and touches no canonical evidence path.

Exercising a refusal path is not evidence that the checking path runs.

## Falsifiers

H51 is refuted, not merely unsupported, if the fresh lineage constructs and validates either later
acquisition without F, if the acquired definitions fall inside M104's exact-context dispatch image,
if F is not required at execution time, if removing F leaves behaviour unchanged, or if hidden-case
generalization succeeds without the acquired feature.
