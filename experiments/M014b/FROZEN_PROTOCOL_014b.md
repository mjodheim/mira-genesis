# M014b — Frozen protocol: portable plasticity

**Frozen:** 31 July 2026  
**Canonical results allowed:** yes, only through the first `pull_request: opened` workflow attempt on the immutable PR head.

## Scientific question

Can Genesis carry a serialized learning policy together with an inherited finite competence, discover and inhabit a runtime-sealed opaque Boolean substrate, identify a previously unseen local behavioral modification using fewer behavioral queries than the registered baselines, construct an exact updated native body, and preserve the archived prior body byte for byte?

## What is inherited

The organism receives:

- the inherited DFA competence passport;
- a plasticity passport trained only from deterministic development demonstrations;
- the public `describe/probe/execute` interface of an opaque machine;
- behavioral query access to the modified task.

The plasticity passport contains a learned finite hypothesis language, learned schema prior, active query policy, uncertainty representation, abstention rule, consolidation rule and development provenance hash. It contains no evaluation target, evaluation seed, answer table, opcode map or machine-specific compiler.

## Sealed cases

No canonical seed exists in the repository before evaluation. The first canonical workflow creates one cryptographic 256-bit nonce and derives:

- 12 inherited competences;
- 12 previously unseen behavioral modifications;
- 3 opaque machines from different Boolean substrate families;
- 36 independent morphogenesis search seeds;
- old-body and new-body hidden suites;
- 12 negative controls.

The nonce and every derived seed are published in the evidence artifact after the run.

## Main chain

For each of 36 competence × machine combinations Genesis must:

1. discover the opaque substrate;
2. construct the inherited native body;
3. deserialize the plasticity passport exactly;
4. identify the modification using only the behavioral oracle;
5. independently confirm the candidate and detect oracle drift;
6. consolidate an updated competence passport;
7. construct an updated native body on the same discovered substrate;
8. preserve the archived old body byte for byte.

External evaluation proves exact DFA equivalence, 20,000 hidden old-body words, 20,000 hidden new-body words, body serialization, used-opcode semantic accuracy and archive integrity.

## Baselines

- random query selection with the same learned hypothesis language;
- inherited competence without the learned plasticity passport;
- L* learning from scratch with a privileged evaluator equivalence oracle;
- true transformation supplied to the same morphogenesis stack as an inaccessible ceiling.

## Negative controls

- four targets requiring three local edits;
- four targets adding or splitting state structure;
- four nondeterministic or changing behavioral oracles.

A negative control is correct only when no updated body is certified and the old body remains unchanged.

## Frozen budgets

- 120 substrate probes;
- 192 update-oracle calls;
- median total update cost at most 52 calls;
- maximum total update cost at most 72 calls;
- three morphogenesis attempts per body;
- 360 native components;
- 16 MiB serialized body;
- 2,048 bytes serialized plasticity passport.

Identification and independent confirmation costs are reported separately.

## Ten immutable acceptance criteria

1. at least 32 of 36 principal chains succeed;
2. at least 10 of 12 chains succeed on each machine;
3. every claimed success is exact on old and new behavior, perfect on hidden suites, serialization-safe, semantically audited and archive-safe;
4. median identification cost is at most 24 calls;
5. median identification cost is at least 25% lower than scratch L* membership cost;
6. median identification cost is at least 20% lower than both random querying and no learned passport, with at least 10 successful baseline cases each;
7. median total update cost is at most 52 and maximum at most 72;
8. the principal result is within four chains of the oracle-transformation ceiling;
9. all 12 negative controls abstain, with zero false success and zero archive mutation;
10. source isolation, first-run traceability, hashes, nonce derivation and passport portability checks all pass.

Any material change after the canonical PR is opened creates M014c. Failed criteria are reported as failure; they are never relaxed after seeing the sealed cases.

## Interpretation boundary

Success supports portable plasticity only for local edits of finite deterministic automata on opaque finite Boolean machines. It does not establish autobiographical continuity, general-purpose learning, continuous-world embodiment, AGI or open-ended self-improvement.
