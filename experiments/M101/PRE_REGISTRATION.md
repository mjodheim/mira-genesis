## H46

Under one fixed authored multi-carrier interpreter, a carrier-neutral transformation-composition
capability acquired from observable demand in a text family, without access to consumer-family
qualification material or a supplied target-operation label, can be registered as persistent lineage
state and then increase constructive reach in fresh record and Python-syntax families relative to an
otherwise identical fresh lineage. The transferred capability can then become causally necessary to
acquire and persist a later Python-syntax transformation derived from observed demand, while the
M100 predecessor state remains conserved, live dependency survives process death, and mutation,
ablation, corruption, rollback and stable replay close their frozen controls.

A positive result would be **bounded cross-family mechanism evidence**. It would not close a
generality gate.

## Why M101 is not M100 with different coefficients

M100 gives the acquisition procedure exact affine target signatures and keeps A, B and C inside one
operation family. M101 changes both dimensions that remain open:

1. the acquisition mechanism sees executable demand evidence rather than a target-operation name or
   numeric signature;
2. the capability acquired because of the producer demand must later operate over carrier families
   that were not available to that acquisition process.

The consumer baseline is not “M100 before another coefficient”. It is an otherwise identical fresh
M101 lineage with the same authored interpreter, atomic operations, public consumer demand, compute
budget and observation budget, but without the producer-domain acquisition in registered state.

## Representation families

The complete M101 qualification uses three new carrier families plus fresh M100 conservation worlds.

### Producer family — text

Values are scalar strings. The inherited catalog contains independently useful atomic text
transformations, but the T0 candidate language may invoke at most one atomic transformation per
candidate. The producer trigger requires two ordered effects; no single inherited atomic operation
may satisfy all of its public and hidden cases.

The acquisition process sees only the producer trigger's **public executable demand evidence** and
the carrier-neutral extension substrate. It must not receive the strings `compose`, `composition`,
`chain`, the expected instruction body, a target-operation identifier, or any record/syntax pool
entry.

### Consumer family — records

Values are exact structured mappings. Atomic record transformations operate on mapping structure and
are distinct from the text atomics. Every transfer world requires two ordered effects and must be
structurally unreachable when a candidate may invoke only one atomic transformation.

### Consumer family — Python syntax

Values are Python source/AST structures. Atomic syntax transformations operate on source structure;
success is checked from the produced source and, where the world declares executable behaviour,
from isolated execution rather than syntax alone.

The direct-transfer worlds require two ordered effects. The later cumulative demand requires three
ordered effects and produces a new registered syntax operation B whose definition must contain live
content-addressed references to the previously acquired generic capability A.

## The acquired producer capability A

The expected semantic class is a generic two-stage sequential-composition combinator. The experiment
may predict that class in advance; **the acquisition process may not be handed it**.

A must be assembled from a carrier-neutral lower language. The implementation may refine encoding,
but the final protocol must preserve these semantic constraints:

- input values are opaque to the combinator;
- transform parameters are referenced by slots rather than concrete text/record/syntax operation
  names;
- the lower language contains no finished `COMPOSE`, `CHAIN`, text, mapping or AST instruction;
- at minimum the lower language can place the opaque input on a stack, apply a referenced transform
  slot, reorder/duplicate stack values where needed, and return a value;
- acquisition is bounded and exhaustively enumerable;
- candidate selection is shortest accepted symbolic body, then canonical content digest;
- an independent validator re-derives the symbolic call order and generic behaviour without access
  to consumer qualification worlds.

A definition containing carrier tags, class names, field names, mapping keys, source identifiers or
qualification identifiers is a falsifier.

## Cumulative chain

The state chronology is:

```text
T0
  exact M100 predecessor state preserved
  authored M101 atomic catalogs available
  no acquired generic composition capability

producer text demand
  -> derive the missing behavioural requirement from observations
  -> assemble and validate A
  -> register A

T1
  M100 state conserved
  A registered
  -> producer process terminates

fresh consumer processes
  -> reuse A on text holdouts
  -> transfer A to record worlds
  -> transfer A to direct Python-syntax worlds

later Python-syntax demand
  -> derive a three-effect requirement from observations
  -> acquire B using live registered A
  -> register B

T2
  M100 state conserved
  A and B registered
  -> terminate producer
  -> reconstruct and reuse all retained capabilities in fresh processes
```

Building and validating A without registration must leave T0's constructive reach unchanged. B must
be absent without registered A under the frozen language, not merely more expensive to find.

## Complete qualification-population shape

The final population is exhaustive: there is no result-dependent draw, replacement or reroll. It
contains exactly **15 fresh worlds**:

| Role | Family | Count | Purpose |
|---|---|---:|---|
| producer trigger | text | 1 | drives A acquisition from observations |
| producer-family holdout | text | 2 | shows A is not one memorised producer pipeline |
| cross-family transfer | record | 3 | tests A on a different structured carrier |
| cross-family transfer | Python syntax | 3 | tests A on source/AST transformations |
| cumulative B reuse | Python syntax | 3 | tests the later A-dependent acquired operation |
| predecessor conservation | M100 affine runtime | 3 | one fresh world each for M100 A, B and C after T2 |

Every world must contain exactly four public cases and four qualification-only hidden cases. The final
pool must vary names, constants, orderings and irrelevant structure sufficiently that a hard-coded
fixture identity cannot satisfy a family. No M097-M100 qualification world may be reused.

Before the final freeze, authoring preflight may only construct/parse the worlds, verify case counts,
verify that declared source is syntactically valid, and verify that every case can be instantiated.
It may not run M101 acquisition, registration, transfer, baseline, mutation, ablation, rollback or
final execution against the pool.

A separate development fixture may be used to debug apparatus and is permanently excluded from the
15-world population.

## Fresh-lineage baseline

The decisive transfer comparison uses a fresh lineage that receives:

- the exact same authored M101 runtime and atomic catalogs;
- the exact same public consumer demand evidence;
- the same construction bound, candidate budget, observation budget and validation policy;
- the exact same M100 predecessor state;
- **no registered A and no access to A's bytes**.

All eight two-effect holdouts after the producer trigger — two text, three record and three direct
syntax worlds — must be unreachable to the fresh baseline and reachable after A registration. The
final checker must also prove structurally that increasing the search budget without adding a
multi-transform combinator cannot change that result.

## Registration control

A `built_but_not_registered` arm receives the exact accepted A bytes and may execute the validator on
them, but does not add A to lineage state. Its state bytes must remain exactly T0 and consumer reach
must match the fresh baseline.

The later B acquisition must likewise be a state transition: construction alone is not acquisition.

## Live dependency controls

The final state must retain live dependency rather than cached host semantics.

At minimum:

- a digest-valid A mutation that reverses or removes one transform application must break every B
  world whose semantics distinguish the mutation;
- ablating A must make B unusable or fail closed rather than silently substituting host composition;
- ablating B must remove B-world reach while leaving unrelated retained capabilities intact;
- corrupting a registered state digest must fail closed;
- source/AST execution must not contain a hidden host helper implementing the exact acquired pipeline
  outside the registered definitions.

The checker must inspect both stored definitions and runtime behaviour.

## Conservation

M101 must preserve the exact M100 predecessor state bytes/digest through T0, T1 and T2. After T2, the
three fresh predecessor-conservation worlds must still execute M100 subtraction, addition and
weighted addition respectively.

A positive M101 therefore cannot be obtained by replacing the predecessor with a new unrelated
runtime and merely copying its metadata.

## Process boundary

Producer acquisition, registration, baseline, transfer, B acquisition, controls, final reuse and
rollback must run in fresh isolated base-Python processes. The final executable protocol must bind a
minimal capsule and process chronology before qualification.

Scientific consumers may receive only the frozen capsule, allowed serialized lineage state and the
specific world payload for that invocation. The repository must be absent from import/search paths;
no acquisition-development module, pool authoring module, model API, network client or remote
executor may be available to the isolated consumer.

## Fault and rollback

A live fault is injected only after the relevant accepted state has been persisted. The fault must
change behaviour, not merely metadata. Restoring the exact pre-fault bytes must reproduce the exact
accepted state and capability in a new process.

A rollback that normalises bytes, rebuilds state from development code or silently replaces a
mutated operation with a host implementation is a falsifier.

## Stable replay

The final executable protocol must freeze the complete process/location-ephemera projection before
the first canonical run. Process identifiers and temporary paths may be projected only when they are
non-causal; process counts, isolation facts, return codes, state bytes/digests, operation bodies,
search counts, accepted counts, baseline outcomes, mutation/ablation outcomes, qualification results
and rollback equality remain scientific evidence.

If clean replay changes any retained scientific field, the attempt is negative. An overlooked
process-ephemera field may not be removed after verdict to save the attempt.

## Fifteen decisive conditions

A positive verdict requires **all 15** conditions to be computed and true:

1. **P1 — frozen bindings:** the final protocol binds the pre-registration, exact predecessor,
   mechanism, pool, runtime/capsule, checker and stable-projection digests before qualification.
2. **P2 — untouched population:** all 15 fresh worlds and 8 cases per world are complete, valid,
   predecessor-distinct and unexecuted by the M101 scientific mechanism before final freeze.
3. **P3 — demand-only producer evidence:** A acquisition receives only allowed public text demand
   evidence and generic substrate state; it receives no target-operation label or consumer material.
4. **P4 — inherited structural insufficiency:** T0 can invoke at most one atomic transform per
   candidate; a machine-checked closure argument proves two ordered effects are unreachable at any
   unchanged search budget.
5. **P5 — genuine carrier-neutral acquisition:** A is exhaustively assembled from lower
   microinstructions, independently validated, free of carrier/fixture identifiers and not selected
   from a finished composition primitive.
6. **P6 — registration is causal:** built-but-unregistered A leaves T0 byte-identical and leaves
   constructive reach equal to the fresh baseline; registration alone creates T1.
7. **P7 — cross-family reach:** retained A solves all 2 text, 3 record and 3 direct-syntax holdouts
   after producer death; the otherwise identical fresh baseline solves 0/8.
8. **P8 — capability not budget:** more search over unchanged T0 cannot close any of those eight
   worlds, consistent with the structural closure proof.
9. **P9 — later demand-derived acquisition:** the later syntax target is recovered from observations,
   B is absent without registered A, and B is acquired and registered only from T1.
10. **P10 — live transitive dependency:** B stores live A references; digest-valid semantic mutation
    or ablation of A breaks the dependent B behaviour without corrupting unrelated retained state.
11. **P11 — cumulative conservation:** state grows only through declared registrations; exact M100
    predecessor bytes and A are conserved through T2, and all three fresh M100 conservation worlds
    still execute correctly after B exists.
12. **P12 — hard process persistence:** producer processes terminate completely; subsequent transfer,
    B reuse and predecessor reuse occur in fresh isolated consumers reconstructed from allowed state
    and the frozen capsule.
13. **P13 — fail-closed controls:** A/B ablation, state corruption and forbidden hidden-host shortcut
    checks fail closed in the direction precommitted by the final protocol.
14. **P14 — live fault and exact rollback:** an accepted-state semantic fault suppresses the
    dependent capability and byte-exact restoration reproduces the accepted state and behaviour in a
    new process.
15. **P15 — stable local replay:** independent clean replay changes no retained scientific field;
    canonical qualification records zero model, network and remote-execution calls, uses no reroll,
    and never overwrites the first result.

## Falsifiers

The attempt is negative if any decisive condition fails. In particular, it is falsified if:

- the producer acquisition is handed an explicit composition target or reads a consumer pool;
- A contains a carrier-specific or fixture-specific shortcut;
- any consumer two-effect world is reachable in T0 under the unchanged language;
- extra search budget substitutes for A without changing the language;
- validated-but-unregistered A changes lineage reach;
- the fresh baseline has A bytes, a larger budget, different atomics or different public evidence;
- A works on text but not every frozen record/syntax transfer world;
- B is reachable without A, does not retain live A dependency, or survives a decisive A mutation via
  a host shortcut;
- M100 predecessor bytes change or M100 A/B/C cease to execute after T2;
- a scientific consumer imports project/development modules outside the frozen capsule or can read
  the repository;
- mutation, ablation or corruption silently repairs itself rather than failing closed;
- rollback restores semantic equivalence but not the exact declared bytes;
- stable replay requires deleting a field not frozen as ephemera before the canonical run;
- model, network or remote-execution competence enters Track A qualification;
- the first result is overwritten, rerolled or repaired after verdict.

## Claim boundary

Even a 15/15 positive result would **not** establish:

- AGI, consciousness or general intelligence;
- open-ended or unrestricted recursive self-improvement;
- arbitrary self-modification;
- an endogenous objective generator;
- elimination of the authored generic interpreter or lower micro-language;
- self-hosting;
- natural-world or independent cross-domain generality;
- closure of G4 or any other generality gate;
- independent human reproduction or external adversarial audit;
- production, repository, credential, network or deployment authority.

The three M101 carrier families, their atomic operation catalogs, their cases, the lower assembly
substrate and the evaluator remain project-authored. A positive result may support only a narrower
statement: **one retained, demand-induced generic capability transferred across authored
representation families and became causally necessary to a later acquired operation under the frozen
bounds.**
