# M052 series closure and next construction boundary

## Decision

M052 is the terminal experiment in the M049–M052 bounded migration-strategy series.

M049 selected one record from a frozen family. M050 composed frozen primitives in a fixed three-stage grammar. M051 allowed a bounded variable-length transformation prefix. M052 removes exact finite-domain behavioral duplicates from that same closed grammar.

These steps are useful qualification and search-efficiency results, but continuing by adding more primitives, greater depth, a larger finite catalogue or another pruning rule would no longer count as a material advance toward the Mira Genesis objective.

## Closed continuation patterns

A successor experiment must not be justified solely by any of the following:

- increasing the number of frozen candidates;
- increasing composition depth;
- adding another human-declared primitive to the same grammar;
- changing ranking, enumeration, caching or pruning while leaving the admissible language fixed;
- repeating the same selection and hidden-validation pattern on another small arithmetic task family;
- describing a larger bounded search as open-ended self-extension.

Such work may remain valid engineering or measurement, but it is outside the active construction frontier.

## Required rupture for M053

The next construction experiment must test endogenous extension of the transformation language.

The continuing lineage must:

1. encounter at least one public task for which every transformation expressible in its accepted language is demonstrably insufficient;
2. diagnose the insufficiency as a limitation of the transformation language or proposal architecture, not merely choose a different existing candidate;
3. construct a new reusable primitive, abstraction, tool or language rule that was not present in the founder catalogue;
4. derive the candidate implementation and its executable tests without access to hidden validation cases;
5. execute the extension in a disposable sandbox under fixed resources;
6. submit the extension to an independent validator with no proposal or adoption authority;
7. adopt the extension transactionally only after validation, preserving body, tools, tests, causal reasons and rollback state;
8. use the acquired extension successfully in a second task family whose solution is not a replay of the first task;
9. preserve a negative or insufficient-evidence episode in which widening is refused;
10. restore the exact accepted state after a forced post-adoption fault and replay the complete lineage deterministically.

## Anti-cheating requirements

M053 does not pass if the supposedly new primitive is selected from a hidden pre-enumerated catalogue, copied from evaluator-only data, encoded as a lookup table of expected answers, or useful only on the task that created it.

The founder may contain a bounded meta-language for describing safe extensions, but the concrete accepted extension and its task-specific structure must be produced after the limitation is observed. The validator must verify novelty relative to the founder language, capability gain under a common resource budget, preservation of inherited behavior and actual reuse on the second task family.

## Claim boundary

This decision changes the active research direction, not the scientific interpretation of M049–M052. M052 remains an exact finite-domain equivalence-pruning result over one frozen grammar. M042 remains the only positive canonical continuous-lineage completion.
