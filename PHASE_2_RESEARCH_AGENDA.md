# Mira Genesis — Phase 2 research agenda

## Starting point

M042 produced the first positive frozen continuous-lineage result satisfying all ten
Genesis completion gates in the bounded deterministic binary-DFA laboratory. That result
is complete within its declared domain and must not be rerun, retuned or silently widened.

Phase 2 asks a different question:

> Which parts of the Genesis mechanism are genuinely domain-general, and which parts were
> consequences of the finite language-acceptor representation used to discover them?

The goal is not to claim open-ended intelligence by increasing state counts or search
budgets. The goal is to move one structural assumption at a time while retaining exact
ground truth, causal controls, immutable first results and reproducible negative findings.

## Phase-2 principles

1. **Change one structural assumption at a time.** The first change is from accept/reject
   languages to reactive input/output transduction.
2. **Keep exact verification as long as possible.** A richer model is useful only while
   equivalence, minimisation and counterexamples remain independently checkable.
3. **Rebuild domain-specific mechanisms.** A renamed DFA encoding is not transfer.
4. **Qualify the rig before freezing a claim.** Formal kernels, generators, ablations and
   release boundaries need permanent falsification tests before any canonical seed exists.
5. **Do not convert bounded success into an AGI claim.** Production authority, external
   credentials, unrestricted code execution and autonomous deployment remain outside the
   scientific sandbox.

## Immediate experiment — M043

M043 qualifies a deterministic Mealy-machine laboratory with finite input and output
alphabets. It must establish:

- canonical representation under state renaming;
- exact minimisation and equivalence;
- shortest distinguishing input words;
- an independent capacity-changing rewrite language;
- constructively available hidden tasks;
- isolated validation and exact rollback;
- discovery of an opaque native representation;
- byte-identical development replay across Python 3.11 and 3.13.

M043 is not a canonical ten-gate rerun. Its purpose is to prove that a later test would be
meaningful rather than a disguised reuse of the DFA machinery.

## Later boundary, not yet authorised

Only after M043 qualification may a separate experiment propose a frozen continuous
Mealy lineage. That later protocol would need new seeds, generators, budgets, controls and
first-result preservation. It may not use M042's positive result to choose any of them.

Moving beyond deterministic finite transducers — for example to timing, stochasticity,
continuous state or unrestricted programs — requires another explicit boundary and a new
verification strategy. None of those steps is implied by M043.

## Parallel measurement track

The proxy-measurement track remains open. M030 and M031 support component-uniform evidence
in two finite grammars, while the distinct optimisation question remains unanswered:
can a pre-written resource-aware adaptive allocation beat that uniform baseline without
recreating M029's allocation failure?

This track should remain separate from M043 so that a better construction result cannot
retroactively validate a measurement policy, and a measurement failure cannot be hidden
inside a larger metamorphosis demonstration.
