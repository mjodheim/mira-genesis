# Genesis Free Metamorphosis v5 — prospective public preregistration

This document records the **prospective** scientific rules for the next Free Metamorphosis campaign before any v5 descendant proposal is evaluated. The real software host, frozen evaluator internals, case-level contracts, source paths and operational artifacts remain private.

## Why a new protocol is needed

Free Metamorphosis v4 produced one retained descendant followed by three hard-pass neutral descendants and was deliberately paused after 4/24 attempts. The v4 record showed that Genesis can perform bounded external selection and heredity, but it also exposed two limitations of the search process:

1. hard-pass neutral variants were preserved as empirical memory but could not become bounded proposal parents;
2. the principal external fitness surface emphasized routing/tool exposure, so a real execution-path improvement could remain invisible if routing did not change.

V5 addresses those limitations prospectively. No v4 result is rescored or reclassified.

## Frozen search semantics

V5 maintains one active **champion** and a bounded frontier of champion-equivalent neutral descendants. A neutral frontier node may become a proposal parent, but it has no selection authority: every resulting descendant must still be compared with the current champion before promotion.

The active frontier is bounded to at most eight neutral nodes and neutral ancestry depth is bounded to two. Parent choice is deterministic: odd-numbered attempts use the champion; even-numbered attempts use an eligible neutral stepping stone when one exists, otherwise the champion.

A promoted champion resets the active neutral frontier around the new baseline. Historical attempts remain in external evidence and the hash-chained record.

## Proposer boundary

The proposer remains external to the experiment host. Genesis generates the attempt prompt deterministically from the frozen control plane and binds the exact prompt, proposal context, selected parent, patch and transcript identities into evidence.

Attempt-specific human steering is forbidden. The external proposer receives only the supplied bundle and aggregate empirical history. It must not use GitHub, the web, connected applications, earlier conversations, saved memory, evaluator internals, hidden challenge cases or external authority copies.

The proposer returns inert files only. Genesis alone decides evaluation, frontier admission and champion promotion.

## Prospective fitness geometry

The v5 observer retains the hard offline build/test/authority boundary and adds two prospectively frozen selection surfaces:

- a wording-family routing surface that tests the same bounded task contracts under multiple fixed conversational formulations;
- deterministic offline execution contracts that distinguish **having an eligible tool** from **successfully executing useful behavior**.

The execution-contract design is informed by the limitation observed in v4 and therefore is **not** independent retrospective confirmation of earlier hypotheses. It is new prospective instrumentation for v5.

The proposer receives aggregate capability scores and totals only. Individual case messages, expected outcomes, fixture details, evaluator logs and hidden authority material are not disclosed.

Cross-runner elapsed time is observational only and is not positive fitness.

## Selection rule

A descendant can become champion only if it:

- passes every hard external boundary;
- has no protected regression relative to the current champion;
- improves at least one frozen capability category by at least one unit **or** reduces the protected diagnostic warning count by at least one;
- remains within the frozen non-test source-growth bound.

A changed descendant that passes the hard boundary and is fitness-equivalent to the champion may enter the bounded neutral frontier. A regressing descendant cannot enter the frontier or become champion.

## Success criterion fixed before the campaign

The v5 campaign will count as its intended cumulative-search milestone only if all of the following occur within the frozen campaign budget:

1. at least **two champion promotions** occur;
2. at least **one promoted champion** has causal proposal ancestry that passes through a descendant that was previously admitted as a neutral frontier node;
3. a later proposer bundle demonstrably starts from the resulting promoted champion, establishing heredity beyond the promotion event;
4. the relevant descendants continue to pass the frozen external hard boundary with no protected regression.

A single promotion, a neutral branch, or a successful development rehearsal is insufficient.

## Claim boundary

Even if the criterion above is met, the supported claim remains bounded: Genesis would have demonstrated cumulative externally selected self-extension on this frozen Mira task surface, including a case where a fitness-neutral stepping stone contributed to a later selected improvement.

This would **not** establish AGI, consciousness, unrestricted self-programming, autonomous production authority, broad cross-project generality or open-ended recursive self-improvement.

## Publication boundary

This public preregistration intentionally does not publish private host source, raw proposals, proposer bundles, campaign-state archives, individual evaluator cases, exact hidden execution fixtures, private source paths, credentials, environment values, raw logs or deployment information.

The private frozen control merge named above is a provenance commitment. It does not authorize disclosure of the private repository or its hidden authority material.
