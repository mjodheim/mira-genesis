# Authors and assistance

## Project author and research director

**Anthony Mets** (`mjodheim`) conceived and directs Mira Genesis, establishes its research
objectives, selects its architecture and authorises its experimental, governance and release
decisions.

At the provenance audit of 12 August 2026, Anthony Mets records that he is the **sole human developer
and research director of Mira Genesis**. The GitHub contributor enumeration observed at that audit is
consistent with this declaration: it exposes the `mjodheim` human account plus repository
automation, with no second human contributor account observed.

The public Git history is the authoritative repository record of attributed changes. The
sole-human-development declaration and contributor enumeration are provenance evidence; neither is,
by itself, a legal conclusion that every individual repository element is copyrightable, patentable
or owned by one person.

These systems are recorded as tools/automation rather than human repository contributors. This
production-process disclosure does not assign authorship, inventorship, ownership or endorsement to
an AI system or its provider and does not attempt to resolve the legal status of every generated
fragment. Provider/account terms and third-party rights remain separate diligence questions.

Anthony Mets makes the project-level human decisions to formulate objectives and protocols, select
or reject proposed implementations, review results, edit/integrate accepted work, and authorise
publication. Material human conception, architecture, experimental design, selection, review,
editing, integration and acceptance evidence should continue to be preserved when relevant to later

The fuller provenance procedure is recorded in

## Future contributors

Externally authored copyrightable contributions are currently subject to the temporary boundary in
[`CONTRIBUTING.md`](CONTRIBUTING.md): they are not accepted for merge unless a separate written
contributor-rights agreement has first been approved for that contributor.

The Developer Certificate of Origin remains a provenance certification and is not a copyright
assignment. Future contributor terms must be chosen deliberately if the project is to preserve
commercial-relicensing and acquisition flexibility.

Issues, reproducibility reports and non-confidential suggestions can still be useful without making
the reporter a code contributor. If sufficiently expressive third-party material is proposed for
incorporation, its rights basis should be resolved before merge.

Additional human contributors should be recorded here if and when an eligible contribution is
accepted. Commit attribution remains part of the provenance record regardless of project-level
credit.
