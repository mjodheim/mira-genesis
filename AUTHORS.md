# Authors and assistance

## Project author and research director

**Anthony Mets** (`mjodheim`) conceived and directed Mira Genesis, established its research
objectives and authorised its experimental and governance decisions. The public Git history is the
authoritative record of changes attributed to the project author and repository automation.

At the IP-boundary audit of 12 August 2026, the GitHub contributor history exposed one human
repository contributor account, `mjodheim`, plus repository automation. This is a provenance
observation, not by itself a legal conclusion about authorship, inventorship or ownership of every
individual element.

## AI-assistance disclosure

Implementation, documentation, testing and experimental analysis were assisted by OpenAI Codex and
other generative tools under the project author's direction where recorded by project provenance.
This disclosure records the production process; it does not assign authorship, inventorship,
ownership or endorsement to a tool or its provider. The legal status of any particular contribution
remains governed by applicable law.

Human conception, architecture, experimental design, selection, review, editing, integration and
acceptance decisions should continue to be recorded when material to later rights diligence.

## Future contributors

Externally authored copyrightable contributions are currently subject to the temporary boundary in
[`CONTRIBUTING.md`](CONTRIBUTING.md): they are not accepted for merge unless a separate written
contributor-rights agreement has first been approved for that contributor.

The Developer Certificate of Origin remains a provenance certification and is not a copyright
assignment. Future contributor terms must be chosen deliberately if the project is to preserve
commercial-relicensing and acquisition flexibility.

Additional human contributors should be recorded here if and when an eligible contribution is
accepted. Commit attribution remains part of the provenance record regardless of project-level
credit.
