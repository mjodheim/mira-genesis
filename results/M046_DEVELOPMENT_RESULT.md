# M046 — positive integrated development result

## Result

**M046 PASSED IN DEVELOPMENT.**

One deterministic Mealy lineage replaced broad exhaustive candidate search with a bounded,
evidence-driven proposal mechanism and completed six accepted adaptive cycles while
preserving independent validation and exact transactional adoption.

The lineage:

1. started from the public two-state M043 founder;
2. faced alternating one-edit and two-edit hidden split-specialisation task families;
3. diagnosed candidate transformations through bounded observations only;
4. generated and ranked a small evidence-backed proposal set;
5. used causal success and failure memory to alter later rankings;
6. submitted proposals to an exact evaluator outside the generator;
7. replayed every admitted trace in the existing disposable M043 validator;
8. adopted six transformations and grew from two to eight states;
9. retained six registered exact tools, six portable learning traces and six journal entries;
10. created a verified combined lineage-and-proposal-memory checkpoint after every cycle;
11. restored the exact version-six combined checkpoint after a forced provisional
    version-seven journal corruption;
12. faced a two-growth terminal challenge outside the fixed one-growth proposal surface;
13. terminated explicitly for insufficient evidence without modifying the accepted body;
14. replayed the complete integrated experiment deterministically.

## Non-exhaustive boundary

The proposal generator did not receive the hidden target body, a witness trace or exact
equivalence access. It constructed only proposals supported by bounded diagnostic
observations. Every cycle recorded a conservative valid candidate-space lower bound, the
number of generated candidates and an explicit marker that the complete candidate space was
not enumerated. The experiment failed closed if the generated set reached the lower bound or
exceeded ten percent of it.

## Resource boundary

The frozen development bounds were:

- at most `128` diagnostic observations per task;
- at most `48` generated candidates per task;
- at most `8` exact task-side validation attempts;
- rewrite depth at most `3`;
- body ceiling `10` states;
- at most `262,144` bytes of working search memory;
- at most `262,144` bytes of persistent causal proposal memory;
- at most `30` seconds of proposal-search wall time per task.

All permanent resource assertions passed.

## Validation separation

Validation remained independent at two levels:

- a task-side exact validator admitted or rejected ranked proposals without exposing its
  hidden target or distinguishing witness to the generator;
- the existing M043 disposable worker independently replayed every admitted trace before the
  existing transactional store could adopt it.

The proposal module contains no exact-equivalence call and no adoption-validator import.

## Interpretation

M046 is the first Mira Genesis development result showing a longer continuous lineage that
selects what transformation to construct from partial evidence without traversing the full
candidate space. It moves the construction frontier beyond the exhaustive regime used by
M042 and M044.

The result remains bounded. The hidden tasks are formal Mealy tasks, the proposal language is
fixed, exact validation is still available and the resource limits are small by software
engineering standards. M046 therefore does not establish open-ended evolution, general
program synthesis, arbitrary architectural self-rewrite, AGI or authority over external
systems.

M046 is not canonical. It selected no seed, froze no hidden bank and authorised no canonical
workflow. M042 remains the only positive canonical continuous-lineage completion.
